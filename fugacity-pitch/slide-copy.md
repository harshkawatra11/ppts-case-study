## Slide 1. Cover

**Wordmark:** PUNARdhatu

**Subtitle:** Punar + Dhatu. Critical minerals recovered from bauxite residue at the refinery gate.

**Abstract (4 lines, factual, no manifesto):**
A staged recovery cascade sited at India's three operating alumina refineries converts red mud,
today an impounded hazardous residue, into iron, titanium dioxide and a scandium and rare earth
concentrate. The plant is built and operated on a tolling basis. The refinery pays for stockpile
reduction, not for a commodity. PUNARdhatu keeps the margin in the fraction the refinery cannot
otherwise recover: 55 parts per million of scandium in every tonne.

**KPI chips (3):**
- `9.17 Mt/yr` red mud generated, 3 named Indian refineries
- `~500 t/yr` contained scandium arising, unrecovered
- `~2 to 3%` global red mud utilisation after four decades

**Team table:**

|---|---|---|
| Anuj Gambhir | (institution) | Member |

**Right column:** labelled block schematic, 4 stages: Reductive roasting and magnetic separation

REE) -> De-alkalisation (Na to Bayer liquor, residue to SCM feedstock). Each block labelled with

**Chip:** FUGACITY 2026, Problem Statement 3, Circular Economy and Waste-to-Wealth

India's three operating alumina refineries impound 9.17 million tonnes of red mud every year, and

**Component (b). Segmented composition bar**, NALCO Damanjodi assay, one tonne basis.
Segments: Fe2O3 52.23%, Al2O3 17.89%, SiO2 5.75%, TiO2 4.88%, Na2O ~4%, CaO ~1.5%, other ~13.75%.
Callout on the sliver: "433 ppm REE, ~55 ppm Sc, inside the 'other' band, drawn at true scale to
show why this fraction is invisible to a bricks-and-cement valorisation route."

**Component (c). Horizontal bar rows with value pills**, utilisation percent by stream.
Fly ash 98% | Steel slag ~60% (est., roadmap stage) | Distillery spent wash high, industry default |
Phosphogypsum minority recycled | **Red mud 2 to 3%, global, 40+ years**

**Component (d). 3 KPI panels:**
- `9.17 Mt/yr` combined arising, NALCO Damanjodi (3.10) + Hindalco Utkal (2.05) + Vedanta Lanjigarh (2.00)
- `pH 10 to 13` residual causticity, the handling hazard behind every pond's closure liability
- `100+ Mt/yr` global generation, ~2 to 3% utilised worldwide

**Component (e). Issue tree**, "why red mud stays impounded":
Root: Red mud stays in the pond
-> Branch 1: Freight economics. Bulk-construction realisations cannot pay to move residue past a
   ~100 to 200 km radius from the pond.
-> Branch 2 (attacked, marked): Value-density loss. A brick permanently discards 52% Fe2O3, 4.9%
   TiO2, 433 ppm REE and 55 ppm Sc in one irreversible step.
-> Branch 3: Alkalinity handling. pH 10 to 13 residue needs de-alkalisation before most reuse
   routes, a step most valorisation proposals skip.

Decides: Refinery plant head, CFO, state pollution control board | Uses: EHS head, corporate
sustainability, downstream alloy and SOFC buyers

## Slide 3. Solution and value proposition

**Headline (action title):**
A tonne of Damanjodi red mud carries 52.2% recoverable Fe2O3 and 55 ppm scandium, and a
bricks-or-road-base route destroys both in one step that cannot be reversed.

**Section chip:** SOLUTION AND VALUE PROPOSITION

**Component (a). KPI hero row, presented with its own constraint attached:**
- `~500 t/yr` contained scandium arising (9.17 Mt x 55 ppm)
- `~80 t/yr` 2025 world Sc2O3 production
- `6x` India's contained arising against total world production, a phasing constraint, not a
  monetisable headline: build-out is staged against demonstrated offtake, not against this figure

**Component (b). Process flow diagram with adjacent stream table:**
Flow: Red mud slurry -> Dewatering (40 to 50% solids) -> Reductive roasting + magnetic separation
-> [Magnetic: Fe sponge/pig iron] + [Non-magnetic: TiO2 route] -> Acid bake/leach -> Solvent
extraction -> Sc2O3 + REE concentrate -> raffinate -> De-alkalisation -> SCM-grade tailing, Na to
Bayer liquor

| Stage | Input | Output | Recovery | TRL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Reduction + magnetic sep. | Red mud cake, Fe2O3 52.2% | Fe sponge / pig iron | Reported up to 71% (lab, pig-iron route) | Pilot to near-commercial |
| 2. Non-magnetic processing | Non-magnetic tailing | TiO2 concentrate | Not independently verified at scale | Lab to pilot |
| 3. Leach + solvent extraction | Residual solids, 55 ppm Sc | Sc2O3 + REE concentrate | Lab-reported 40 to 75% band, process-dependent | Lab to pilot |
| 4. De-alkalisation | Demetallised residue, pH 10 to 13 | SCM feedstock, Na to Bayer liquor | Neutralisation, not a yield metric | Lab to pilot |

**Component (c). Mass-split band**, illustrative, one tonne of feed:
Iron fraction (largest, ballast) | TiO2 fraction (secondary) | Sc/REE fraction (smallest by mass,
margin layer) | Residue to SCM (remainder). Caption: "Mass order and value order run in opposite
directions: the smallest fraction by weight is the highest value per kilogram."

**Component (d). TRL ladder**, five rungs, matching the table in (b): Commercial (dewatering) ->
Pilot to near-commercial (Fe, DRI route, ~300C) -> Lab to pilot (TiO2) -> Lab to pilot (Sc/REE SX)
-> Lab to pilot (de-alkalisation/geopolymer). Caption: "No commercial-scale Sc-from-red-mud plant
identified anywhere in the world. NALCO's own red-mud pilot is explicitly a pilot."

**Component (e). Paired panels:**
What we build: A refinery-gate cascade recovering Fe, TiO2 and Sc/REE, sold as a tolling service.
What we do not build: Bricks, road base, or a standalone REE mine independent of an operating
refinery's own waste stream.

**Source line:** ICSOBA, DRI from red mud | academia.edu, reductive smelting, 71% Fe recovery |
PMC 8779884, Sc extraction by alkali fusion-leaching | mdpi.com 10.3390/app9224902, 1300C threshold

## Slide 4. Go to market

**Headline (action title):**
Three named refineries generating 9.17 million tonnes a year pass through a five-point site screen
into a five-year, module-by-module build-out anchored at NALCO Damanjodi.

**Section chip:** GO TO MARKET

**Component (a). Layered arc funnel:**
`9.17 Mt/yr` arising, 3 named refineries -> `3 sites` pass the 5-point screen (all three qualify)
-> `1 anchor module` Year 1 to 2 -> `3 modules` Year 3 to 5, full Odisha cluster

**Component (b). Numbered badge list, the 5-point site screen:**
1. Annual arising sufficient to amortise a module (Damanjodi's 3.10 Mt/yr is the natural anchor)
2. Existing on-site Bayer/caustic infrastructure to return recovered Na liquor
3. Grid power and process water already present (true at all three operating refineries)
4. Institutional appetite for a liability-transfer or BOO structure (evidenced by NALCO's own
   pilot-plant investment)
5. Proximity to Odisha's industrial-chemical and metallurgical ecosystem for offtake logistics

**Component (c). Staged rollout bar chart, by year:**
Year 1: 1 module (Damanjodi, pilot scale) | Year 2: 1 module (Damanjodi, scaled) | Year 3: 2
modules (+Hindalco Utkal) | Year 4 to 5: 3 modules (+Vedanta Lanjigarh, full Odisha cluster)

**Component (d). 5 stakeholder cards, Needs / Lacks / Gets:**
| Role | Needs | Lacks today | Gets |

| EHS head | Reduced dust, leachate, closure risk | A technical partner for alkalinity/dust | The de-alkalisation step as direct risk reduction |

## Slide 6. Feasibility, risk and impact

**Headline (action title):**
India's approximately 500 tonnes a year of contained scandium is six times the entire 2025 world
production of approximately 80 tonnes, and that gap sets the build-out pace, not the ambition.

**Section chip:** FEASIBILITY, RISK AND IMPACT

**Component (a). Tornado chart, what moves module economics:**
Variables ranked by swing width: Sc recovery % (40 to 75% band) | Sc2O3 price ($1,200 to $5,500+/kg
band) | Reductant/energy cost (DRI vs smelting route) | Throughput utilisation

**Component (b). Base / bull / bear bars:**
Bear: low Sc recovery, controls relax, price reverts toward $1,200/kg | Base: mid-band recovery,
price holds in the post-control $3,500 to $5,500/kg range | Bull: higher recovery, demand grows
toward the 117+ t/yr 2026-27 forecast, controls persist

**Component (c). Claim, confidence, basis list:**
- NCMM funding for unconventional sources is real and allocated: High confidence, PIB/mines.gov.in
- NALCO's red-mud pilot validates technical interest at PSU scale: High confidence, alcircle.com
- Fe recovery at pilot to near-commercial readiness: Medium confidence, lab-reported figures only
- Sc/REE recovery at commercial scale: Low confidence, no plant identified globally, stated openly

**Component (d). Risk register table, 1:1 named mitigations, thin-market risk first:**
| Risk | Level | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Sc market absorption: ~500 t/yr contained vs ~80 t/yr world market | High | Phase build-out against demonstrated offtake, not the contained-resource figure |
| Sc2O3 price reliance on Chinese export controls persisting | Medium-High | BOO/tolling revenue is liability-based, not Sc-price-dependent; Sc is the margin layer, not the sole revenue basis |
| NORM handling, not yet verified for red mud specifically | Medium | Independent radiological assay of feed and streams before scale-up |
| Refinery-partner concentration, only 3 named customers in India | Medium-High | Anchor-then-expand sequencing; international process-licensing as longer-term diversification |
| TRL gap on Sc/REE solvent extraction, lab/pilot only globally | High, stated openly | Sequence commercialisation: Fe revenue de-risks the module while Sc/REE SX scales from pilot |

**Component (e). 6 impact boxes, each with a figure and a unit:**
CO2e: framework stated (avoided virgin production minus process energy), net figure marked "to be
quantified at pilot stage" | Hazardous waste diverted: tonnes processed per module, direct function
of throughput | Water: pond-related groundwater risk reduced by de-alkalisation | Import
substitution: against 93% China-sourced rare-earth magnet imports, FY24-25 | Land: avoided new pond
footprint per tonne processed | Replicability: >100 Mt/yr global red-mud problem, same cascade

**Component (f). NOW / NEXT / LATER 3-horizon strip:**
