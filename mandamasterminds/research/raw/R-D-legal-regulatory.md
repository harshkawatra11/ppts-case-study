**Implication for the deal**
- The 3-year test looks at the three financial years preceding the commitment. For a commitment in FY27 (now), those are FY24 (loss), FY25 (loss) and FY26 (profit), so One97 FAILS the net-profit condition if Liminal's Singapore entity counts as "financial services". Earliest clean window on this test is FY30 (after FY27, FY28, FY29 profits), unless RBI approval is sought.
- Escape route: VDA custody is not regulated by an Indian "financial sector regulator" (FIU-IND is an AML unit, not RBI/SEBI/IRDAI/PFRDA), so there is an arguable position that a pure custody entity is NOT "financial services" under the Explanation. This is untested; AD banks may still refer it to RBI. Investing in the Indian entity (FAIT) instead is FDI-side domestic investment and avoids ODI entirely.
- Any residual ED/FEMA investigation against One97 could trigger the NOC requirement for any ODI leg; plan 60 days for deemed NOC.
