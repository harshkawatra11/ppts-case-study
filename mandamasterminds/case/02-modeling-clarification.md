# Modeling Clarification

Source file: `mandamasterminds/74d06c84-6cb8-4e28-bbed-cc087fd01a08.pdf` (1 page). Converted
verbatim. Requirement IDs in bold (K1 to K5) are added for cross-referencing.

---

- **K2** The original Project Satoshi Gate case document remains the governing source of truth for
  all case facts, transaction terms, constraints, and stated modeling inputs.
- **K1** Where the case does not specify a parameter necessary to evaluate one of the proposed
  transaction structures or scenarios, teams may make reasonable assumptions, provided those
  assumptions are clearly disclosed in their presentation. Teams should not replace, override, or
  substitute external figures for any inputs expressly provided in the case.
- **K3** Any proposed financing structure, minority/JV economics, valuation methodology,
  earn-out/CVR structure, WACC, or other unstated parameter should be treated as a team assumption
  rather than a case fact.
- **K4** Teams should include a dedicated assumptions slide listing all material assumptions used in
  their financial model
- **K5** and should show breakeven on both an accounting (PAT) and cash (FCF) basis.
