No study finds availability above 80%. Missing antivenom matters: India records 58,000 snakebite deaths a year.
Root cause: stock is recorded, but never seen or moved district-wide
  • Seen late: HMIS reports monthly, after the shelf empties
  • No forecast: burn rates, seasons and surges go uncounted
  • Not moved: surplus at a nearby PHC goes unseen
  • Not rotated: near-expiry stock never reaches busier PHCs
  • Hiring is slow: 79.9% of CHC specialist posts sit empty

2Solution: one district view that forecasts, flags and proposes, and a named officer decides

1Sense
Stock, receipts and issues from existing state exports
2Forecast
P50 and P90 demand for each facility and medicine
3Flag
Stock-out risk in the resupply window and units set to expire
4Propose
Sized transfer, reorder or rotation, with reasons
5Approve
A named officer approves, edits or rejects every move
6Learn
Receipt is confirmed; outcomes sharpen the next forecast
Officer approves every move Stock counts only, no patient data State data stays with the state Hindi and English voice for PHC staff Precedent: eVIN cut vaccine stock-outs 80% once stock became visible in 733 districts.

3Approach: each PS6 requirement mapped to a method and an output

PS6 requirementMethodWhat the officer sees
R1 · Demand forecast per facilityConsumption rebuilt from the stock ledger (opening + receipts − closing). TSB intermittent-demand method for sparse items; a LightGBM quantile model on recent use, season and calendar for the rest.P50 and P90 demand; days of cover as a range, e.g. 4.2 days (2.9 at P90)
R2 · Stock-out and expiry riskChance that P90 demand over the resupply lead time exceeds stock on hand; a first-expiry-first-out batch projection of units still on the shelf at each expiry date.A risk band, plus units and ₹ value at risk of expiry
R3 · Seasons, outbreaks, sudden changeMonsoon, heat and festival calendar features; IDSP weekly syndromic counts and IMD rain and heat as surge inputs; a CUSUM change-point test on recent consumption.The surge raises the forecast itself, and the trigger is named
R4 · Transfers and reorder quantitiesMin-cost-flow over the district road network (OSRM) that minimises stock-out days, value expired and transport cost. Donors keep 14 days of cover; cold-chain items travel cold; reorders follow an order-up-to rule at P90 lead-time demand.Ranked moves, each with quantity, source, km and minutes
R5 · Sparse, irregular, incomplete dataPooling across similar facilities and medicines for thin histories; freshness flags on stale entries; outlier clipping; gaps filled from stock deltas, never invented.Confidence that drops when data is thin or stale
R6 · Explain every recommendationThe engine writes reason codes (cover, P90 demand, expiry date, donor surplus, distance) and a stated confidence score. Language models only phrase them in Hindi or English; they never compute a number.One plain-language reason line on every proposal
Stack: Python and FastAPI · LightGBM and statsmodels · OR-Tools · OSRM road routes · Next.js dashboard · Firestore · Sarvam voice (saaras STT, Sarvam 105B LLM, bulbul TTS) for Hindi, Hinglish and English · Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite for typed answers

TestHow we measure it, and the target

Stock-outs and expiryReplay held-out months with and without the engine's moves; fewer stock-out days and less ₹ expired than baseline
