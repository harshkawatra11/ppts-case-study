## Conflicts

- **Remand timeline (B01, B16):** one AI search summary (not independently verified by this verifier against a primary filing) stated remand proceedings began 17 October 2025 with a DGTR Disclosure Statement on 12 May 2026, while a separate June 2026 industry circular said the final remand findings were still awaited at that point. Both cannot be fully reconciled from the sources retrieved; only the 12 February 2025 and 3 August 2026 dates are independently corroborated across multiple sources.
- **What the Calcutta High Court actually quashed (B19, B22):** the CBIC-instruction-based summary (taxguru.in) and European Coatings both describe the court as quashing the May 2025 Customs notification, while a Trilegal quarterly summary states the court also quashed DGTR's underlying final findings of 12.02.2025 for procedural irregularity. Both versions are kept; the research document should flag that secondary sources differ on whether the DGTR findings themselves, or only the Customs notification implementing them, were quashed.
- **Dahej plant company attribution (B29, B30):** a BSE filing (via search summary) attributes a 16,500-to-33,000 MTPA Dahej, Gujarat TiO2 expansion to a context this verifier associated with Kilburn Chemicals, while an ICIS headline and a Ken Research summary attribute a near-identical 2023 doubling (to 33,000 tonnes/year) at Dahej to Meghmani Organics Limited ("MOL"). The two near-identical capacity figures cannot both be independently confirmed as separate facts; it is more likely they describe the same Meghmani Organics plant and the Kilburn attribution in the earlier search pass was wrong. The research document should use the Meghmani Organics attribution and flag the Kilburn figure as unconfirmed.
- **ECHA de-listing date (B55):** sources give 29 August 2025 and 11 September 2025 for when ECHA removed the TiO2 carcinogen classification from its Classification and Labelling Inventory. Both dates are kept; neither was confirmed against the ECHA C&L Inventory directly.
- **EU E171 ban effective dates (B58):** sources disagree on whether the ban took effect in stages from early 2022 with a 6-month phase-out, or as an import ban from January 2022 followed by a sales ban from June 2022. Both versions are kept pending a check of the final Commission regulation text.
- **India's TiO2 demand denominator for the 57% China-import-share figure (B07, B08):** the 57% figure is tied to the DGTR's period-of-investigation import volume (226,869 tonnes) against an unspecified total Indian demand figure, while a separate figure in the same article gives Indian rutile-sulphate-grade demand alone as "near 300,000 tonnes," a different and narrower base. These two denominators should not be conflated in the research document.
