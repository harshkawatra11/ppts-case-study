## Gaps

- No clean, single, dated raw-material (or TiO2-specific) cost line as a percentage of sales was found for FY24, FY25 or FY26 in any source opened (A10, A57). The research document should rely on the model's own assumption (A23 in the model register) rather than an unverified aggregator figure.
- No current (FY24-FY26) management statement on TiO2 price inflation specifically, from a primary earnings call or investor presentation, was located; all TiO2-cost statements found are from 2011, 2019 or 2022 (A53 to A56).
- The FY2024-25 ESG Performance Review PDF and the company's own environmental-performance webpage both returned HTTP 404 on direct fetch (A51); Scope 1/2/3 figures rest on secondary summaries only and should be re-verified against the primary BRSR document (not located at a stable URL in this pass) before being quoted in the research document.
- No net-zero commitment or target year for Asian Paints was found (A50).
- Decorative market-share percentages for all players (Asian Paints, Birla Opus, Berger, Kansai Nerolac, Indigo) are inconsistent across secondary sources and none trace to an audited company disclosure or an independent syndicated market-share report (A61 to A67). Any market-share number used in the research document should be presented as a range with its specific source named inline, not as a single point figure.
- Indigo Paints has no decorative-segment share figure at all in the sources opened (A66).
