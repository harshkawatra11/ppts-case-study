## Conflicts

1. The plan's seed list names "Schulz and Keddie 2018" for drying-stratification suppression under high viscosity. The paper actually located under that description is Schulz, Brinkhuis, Crean, Sear and Keddie, Soft Matter, with a 2022 date, not 2018 (see D42). A separate, genuine 2018 Soft Matter item exists, but it is a review article on stratification generally (D43), not a Schulz-and-Keddie-authored original paper. Both are kept here: the 2022 original-research paper under the Keddie group's name, and the 2018 review, since neither single item fully matches the plan's citation as given.
2. US5147940A is widely cited (including in some secondary sources) as a "Rohm and Haas patent," but the patent's own front page lists Union Oil Company of California as the original assignee at filing in 1991, with Rohm and Haas appearing only via a later 1992 assignment record (D01). Both the original-assignee fact and the later-assignment fact are kept, since the "Rohm and Haas" framing in the plan is not simply wrong, just imprecise about which point in the patent's history it refers to.
