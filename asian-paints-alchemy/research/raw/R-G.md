## Conflicts

- Telangana Cool Roof Policy end year/target split: one source (plaksha.edu.in, G10) gives 200 sq km (Hyderabad) and 100 sq km (rest of state) as a 2028-29 target reached by yearly doubling from 2023-24; Deccan Herald (G11) attributes the same 200/100 sq km figures to 2030. The headline 300 sq km by 2028 figure (G09) is consistent across both, but the sq km split and its exact target year are not.
- Telangana cool roof electricity savings: iamrenew.com (G12) reports the policy mitigates "around 20%" of extra cooling energy cost, with no GWh figure given; a separate secondhand account (G13, source page itself returned HTTP 403 on direct fetch) states a projected saving of 600 million units (GWh) a year after five years. The two figures are not obviously reconcilable and G13 could not be independently verified.
- Ahmedabad Heat Action Plan mortality impact: one estimate credits the plan with preventing nearly 2,300 premature deaths; a separate study (attributed to Dr. Jeremy Hess) credits it with averting about 1,190 deaths per year. These are different metrics (a cumulative total versus an annual rate) and are not necessarily contradictory, but they are reported in secondary coverage as if comparable, so both are kept (G16).
- ECBC cool-roof reflectance/emittance threshold: one secondary source (G35, quoting what it presents as the code text) gives solar reflectance 0.70 and emittance 0.75 for roofs under 20 degrees slope; another secondary source mirroring a BEE training deck (G36) gives 0.60 and 0.90 for the same category, citing ASTM E903-96 and ASTM E408-71. Search evidence suggests 0.70/0.75 is the older ECBC 2008 figure and 0.60/0.90 is ECBC 2017's lowered threshold (G37), but this was not confirmed against either primary ECBC PDF.
- Data-centre cooling's share of total energy use: figures range from about 23% (one cited LBNL study of 12 facilities, G44) to 30-40% (a widely cited Electronics Cooling breakdown, G42) to about 40% "in inefficient facilities" (G43), to about 50% of IT load specifically, not total power (G45). These use different denominators and different facility samples and should not be collapsed into a single number in the deck.
