## Gaps

- The model's own benchmark check C1 (m=1.55, x=5.213, Qsca within 0.1 percent of 3.10543, citing Bohren and Huffman) could not be independently confirmed against a public, citable page; the value most plausibly sits in the Bohren and Huffman textbook's own worked table, which is not available online in full text in this search. Recommend a direct run of `miepython.mie(1.55, 5.213)` as the independent check, logged with its numeric output, rather than a literature citation.
- No public cie.co.at CSV table was reachable in this pass, so the model's embedded 10 nm D65 and CIE 1931 observer arrays were not cross-checked for the greater-than-0.5-percent mismatch the plan calls for. This needs either a direct cie.co.at account-gated download or a secondary package (colour-science, colorSpec) cross-check logged with its own citation.
- The Tiarks dependent-scattering reference named in PLAN.md's seed list was not locatable under that name; only Fitzwater and Hook (1985) and the Auger-Stout exchange were found.
- The 25 to 45 percent porosity-of-pigment-layers-on-paper figure that assumption A05 rests on was not independently confirmed from a paper-coatings-specific source; general CPVC literature confirms the mechanism (porosity rises above CPVC) but not this specific numeric range for paper coatings.
- Exact k(lambda) values for rutile at 380, 400 and 420 nm were not found tabulated in an openly accessible source; only band-gap and qualitative edge-shape evidence was found.
- The Saunderson correction's exact K1 and K2 numerical formulas sit inside the paywalled ISO 18314-2:2023; only a patent's plain-language description of what K1 and K2 represent was retrieved.

## Conflicts

- Three different Asian Paints technical data sheets give three different per-coat dry film thickness figures for premium interior emulsion products: Apcolite Premium Emulsion (India, professionals TDS) 30 micron per coat (C32); Apcolite Premium (UAE sheet) minimum 35, typical 50, maximum 60 micron (C34); Royale Luxury Emulsion Silk (Oman) 30 to 50 micron, typical 40 micron (C35). These are different products and different regional markets, not the same product measured three ways, so all three are kept rather than resolved to one figure; the model's own assumption (A01/A02, outside this lane) should be checked against the India-market Apcolite sheet (C32/C33) as the most directly comparable.
- ISO 6504-3 has two incompatible editions in circulation: the current 2019 edition (hiding power by a Y/Y10 tristimulus method, C38) and the withdrawn 2006 edition (contrast ratio method at a fixed 20 m2/l spreading rate, C39). The model and research document use "contrast ratio" as their working concept, which matches the withdrawn 2006 method's language, not the 2019 edition's; this is noted, not resolved, since both editions are real published standards.
