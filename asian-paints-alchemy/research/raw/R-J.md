## Gaps

1. The Dow dow.com opacifiers page (J03, J04) gives the "20-35%" TiO2-reduction figure and the spacing description as reported through a fetch-and-summarise pass rather than a raw-HTML word-for-word capture; the spacing quote in J03 is confirmed as the tool's direct quotation, but the "20-35%" figure in J04 should be re-read directly from the live page before being cited as an exact phrase in the research document.
2. The Chemours Ti-Pure TS-6300 calculator page (J06) returned HTTP 403 Forbidden to direct WebFetch; the 4.93 kg CO2e/kg TDMA industry-average figure rests on a WebSearch snippet of that page's own footnote text, not a direct read of the live page. A human reader with browser access should open `https://www.tipure.com/en/support/6300-calculator` directly to confirm the figure and its exact wording before it is used as a locked number anywhere in the research document.
3. No TDMA (Titanium Dioxide Manufacturers Association) first-party statement was located directly on a tdma.info page; the only TDMA figure found is as cited secondhand inside Chemours' own calculator (J06). This matches the plan's lane B seed note that TDMA material should be checked directly; it was not accessible in this pass.
4. No peer-reviewed journal LCA paper reporting pigment-grade (sulfate- or chloride-route) TiO2's cradle-to-gate CO2 footprint in kg CO2e per kg, with a verbatim quotable sentence, was located. The closest peer-reviewed candidate found, Middlemas, Fang and Fan's life cycle assessment of an alkaline-roasting TiO2 route (reporting about 7.47 kg CO2 per kg TiO2 for that process), covers an emerging process, not the sulfate or chloride routes that dominate current production, and was not opened directly to pull a verbatim sentence; it is not included as a row above because no verbatim snippet was confirmed. Caramazana-Gonzalez et al. 2017 (Green Chemistry) was found covering nano-TiO2 synthesis routes (GWP from under 12 to 86 kg CO2-eq per kg depending on precursor), which is a different product (nanoparticle, not pigment-grade) and so is also not included as a row.
5. On the third remaining target (an Asian Paints management statement on TiO2 or raw-material costs): verifier V-A's lane A search (`research/raw/R-A.md`, row A57 and its Gaps section) already confirmed that no current (FY24-FY26) Asian Paints management statement specifically on TiO2 price or raw-material cost inflation, sourced to a primary earnings call or investor presentation, could be found; the only TiO2-cost statements V-A located are dated 2011, 2019 and 2022 (rows A53 to A55). This lane's own search did not find a more current cost-specific statement either, which confirms that gap rather than closing it. This lane did, however, independently find a current (2026-accessed) Asian Paints statement on TiO2 itself, on scattering efficiency and cradle-to-gate carbon footprint rather than cost (J07, above), which is offered as the closest available current substitute for the deck or research document if a current, dated, on-topic Asian Paints TiO2 quote is needed.

## Conflicts

1. J04's "20-35%" TiO2-reduction figure for Dow's opacifier category differs from the figure independently found and logged by verifier V-D for the related EVOQUE product specifically ("up to 20% less TiO2," R-D row D11, sourced from a 2011 Coatings World article on EVOQUE's launch). Both figures are kept: D11 is a narrower, older, product-specific claim for EVOQUE alone; J04 is a newer, broader claim covering Dow's opacifier/opaque-polymer line as a whole, including ROPAQUE. The two are not necessarily describing the same product or test basis, so they are not reconciled into one number.
