## Gaps

- No published water-evaporation-rate figure (micron/second or equivalent) for a fresh interior paint film under Indian climate zones was located to check the model's A29, A30 and A31 directly; IMD's own climatological tables report temperature and rainfall, not film-drying evaporation rates, so this gap is structural to the available public data, not a search failure (F38).
- No BIS clause-level contrast-ratio or hiding-power limit was confirmed for IS 428 (washable distemper) because the two direct-fetch attempts at BIS and ANSI source documents failed (404 and 403); only secondary summaries were available (F29, F30).
- No current, India-specific, quantified premium/mid/economy value-share breakdown of the decorative paint market was found (F46); all tier statements located were qualitative.
- Value Exterior Emulsion's raw PDF text could not be independently re-opened as readable text (WebFetch returned only binary); its figures in F12 rest on the WebSearch engine's own summary of that PDF, not a verifier-read original, and are marked "VERIFIED (search only)" accordingly.
- Several PDF fetches (IS 5410 duplicate pages, Tractor Emulsion Nepal sheet, ISO 6504-3:2006 ANSI preview, BIS SR428) returned unreadable binary or HTTP errors; where this blocked an answer it is marked UNVERIFIABLE above rather than estimated.

## Conflicts

- Asian Paints' market-share figure conflicts across sources: about 34 percent of the total paints market (recent, undated figure from a search summary) versus 53 percent of the decorative-paints segment specifically (2015 analyst note reported by Business Standard). The two figures use different bases (total market vs. decorative-only) and different years, so both are kept rather than reconciled (F43).
- Decorative paints' share of the overall Indian paints and coatings market is reported differently by different sources: about 70 to 75 percent (one 2019 Indian Paint Association-sourced estimate) versus "more than three-quarters" (an undated Phillip Capital sector note). Both are kept (F41, F42).
- Several current Asian Paints technical data sheets fetched for this lane (Royale Luxury Matt via the Kadisco distribution channel, and the Value Exterior Emulsion sheet per its search summary) direct the installer to contact a "Berger Paints Sales Representative" for an alternate paint system, even though the sheet is branded Asian Paints or Kadisco Asian Paints. This is noted as an anomaly in the source material itself (consistent with Asian Paints' recent move to a combined "Asian Paints Berger" branding seen on the Apcolite Premium Emulsion sheet, F01) rather than a conflict between two independent facts, so it is not listed as a row-level correction.
