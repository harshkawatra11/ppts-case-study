# Critique of alchemy-whiteness-research.md

Critic: CR. Scope: the research document only, scored against the case-competition-deck skill's
critique_rubric.md, re-based per PLAN.md section 8 for a research deliverable rather than a
100-slide M&A deck. No locked number, the case choice, or an H2 title is proposed for change
anywhere below; every fix is a tag correction, a cross-reference correction, or a row-count note.

## 0. What was read

PLAN.md sections 3, 4 and 8 in full. The case-competition-deck skill's SKILL.md and
references/critique_rubric.md. case/01-briefs.md in full. model/model_tables.md in full.
research/alchemy-whiteness-research.md in full, all 3,514 lines, including Appendix A
(bibliography), Appendix B (model tables reproduced), Appendix C (glossary) and Appendix D
(data conflicts and method limits). A word and table count check and a placeholder-text sweep
were run directly against the file.

Measured facts used throughout this critique: the document runs 89,611 words and carries 185
pipe tables (plan minimum: 70,000 words, 130 tables), carries zero em dashes or en dashes and
zero exclamation marks, and a sweep for TODO, Insert, Placeholder, TBD, Lorem and names from
other case decks (SwiftKart, HarvestBox, Paytm, Liminal, Setu) returned no matches.

## 1. The two strongest things

First, the document's honesty discipline is carried all the way through, not just stated once.
The same four-part honest reading (the Bruggeman-mix dependency, the no-flocculation
simplification, the never-measured-on-a-panel statement, and the unconfirmed-duty-status
statement) appears, consistently worded, in section 4's locked numbers, section 16.6, section
17.2a, section 21.3, section 21.5 and Appendix D.11, and every one of the model's thirteen
checks (Table T3) is reproduced and explained rather than merely cited. A judge who presses on
whether the 39 percent figure is a measurement gets the same honest answer no matter which
section they press on.

Second, the twelve-design screen in section 12 (Table T8) is genuinely symmetric and genuinely
falsifies the brief's own suggested conventional routes on the brief's own named terms: barium
sulfate, the brief's explicitly named conventional approach (W-S5), is shown as the single worst
performer of all twelve designs under the identical test applied to the document's own proposal,
and the "0 of 6" framing (zero substitute pigments or particle-level air designs reach premium
brightness while saving a competitively large share of TiO2) is the clearest, most quotable
version of the contrarian-recommendation pattern found anywhere in the document.

## 4. Issues

### Issue 1. "Exhibit for the deck" pointers disagree with the actual slide blueprint

**Section.** Sections 9, 11 and 13 (the per-section exhibit lines), against section 27.2 (the
slide blueprint table).

**Current state.** Section 9's exhibit line states Table T4 is "the physics behind slide 1/5
('THE TECHNOLOGY') and the moneyshot card on slide 4/5." Section 13's exhibit line states Table
T5 "is the direct evidence behind slide 3/5's 'CONCLUSIVELY' verdict." Section 11's exhibit line
states the two verified third-party quotes "feed slide 2/5's and slide 4/5's third-party
validation callouts." Section 27.2's own slide blueprint table, built later in the same document
specifically to be the authoritative mapping, instead places T4 on slide 4 (ribbon position 2/5,
panel THE PHYSICS), places T5 on slide 4 (ribbon position 2/5, panel AIR GAP), and places both
third-party quotes on slides 5 and 6 only, with none on slide 4 (ribbon position 2/5).

**Fix.** Reconcile the exhibit lines in sections 9, 11 and 13 against section 27.2's table, since
section 27.2 is later and more specific and should be treated as the one the deck-derivation step
(PLAN.md section 15.4) actually follows. Either edit the three exhibit lines to match section
27.2, or, if section 27.2 is the one that drifted during a later revision, edit section 27.2
instead; either direction is a pure cross-reference fix and touches no locked number, no H2
title and no slide count.

**Why it matters.** PLAN.md section 15.4 and touchpoint F both instruct the deck-derivation step
to read these per-section exhibit lines as inputs. A main agent deriving the deck slide by slide
could legitimately pull T4 onto slide 3 on section 9's instruction while section 27.2 (the
document's own blueprint, and the more detailed one) places it on slide 4; the two instructions
cannot both be followed, and the gap would only surface once Stage 2 starts building.

### Issue 2. The [T12] tag does not resolve for the binder-mass and binder-saving figures

**Section.** Section 2.4 (the moneyshot table) and section 16.5 (the headline-saving arithmetic).

**Current state.** Both sections report a binder saving of 41.6 g to 24.1 g per m² (42 percent)
and tag it `[T12]`. The printed T12 table, in model_tables.md and in the research document's own
Appendix B, carries only TiO2 mass, materials cost and CO2e columns for each scenario; it does
not carry a binder-mass column. A reader who follows the `[T12]` tag to check the binder figure,
exactly as the tag legend in section 0.2 instructs, will not find it in the table the tag points
to.

**Fix.** Either add a binder-mass column to T12 in model_tables.md (requires rerunning the model
and is the main agent's call under PLAN.md 5.3, not this critic's to direct), or, with no model
rerun needed, retag the two binder figures `[M]` (a model output, resolving to
model_outputs.json per the same tag legend) rather than `[T12]`, in both section 2.4 and section
16.5. The second option is the lower-cost fix and changes no number.

**Why it matters.** The document's own stated standard (PLAN.md section 2.3, restated in research
section 0.2) is that every tag names "exactly where" a number can be checked. This is the one
number in the 25-number spot-check that failed that standard, and it sits in the moneyshot table,
the single panel most likely to be challenged by a judge in the room.

### Issue 3. Lane E's own raw file falls short of the plan's stated row-count floor

**Section.** Section 0.7 (verification tally per lane).

**Current state.** Section 0.7's own table reports Lane E (materials, production and handling) at
2 VERIFIED, 22 VERIFIED (search only), 0 CORRECTED and 2 UNVERIFIABLE, a total of 26 rows.
PLAN.md section 6 states every lane's raw file should carry "35 to 70 rows a lane." Lane E is the
only one of the ten lanes below that floor; every other lane's own row total (computed the same
way from section 0.7's own numbers) sits inside the 35-to-70 range.

**Fix.** Dispatch a short follow-up pass on `research/raw/R-E.md` to add rows until Lane E clears
35, following the same schema and status conventions as the other nine lanes, then update section
0.7's own tally to match. This is a verifier-lane extension, not a change to any number already
used in the document; none of the document's cost or production claims in sections 14, 18 or 20
would need to change, since the gap is in row count, not in a wrong fact.

**Why it matters.** Lane E backs the seal-coat latex specification (section 14.5), the PCC
production route (section 18.1) and the raw-material handling table (section 18.5), three of the
document's most production-facing, most-likely-to-be-cross-questioned sections. A thinner lane
behind exactly those claims is a specific, nameable gap, not a general concern, and the
document's own section 0.7 table is what exposes it.

### Issue 4. About half the H2 titles are topic labels, not number-led findings

**Section.** Sections 0, 1, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 17, 19, 22, 24, 26, 27 and 29 (title text), re-
based per PLAN.md section 8 against the skill's action-title dimension applied to all 30 H2
titles rather than a 10-slide sample.

**Current state.** Titles such as "The model: Mie theory, crowding, two-coat optics and colour in
one pipeline" (§10), "Where it applies: interior emulsions first, then exteriors, industrial and
automotive" (§19) and "Assumptions register and model summary" (§26) describe what the section
covers rather than stating a finding with a number, unlike §2, §9, §16 or §20, which lead with a
tagged figure. Scored on the skill's own point system (1 point for a full-sentence finding, 1
point for a number, per title), roughly half of the 30 titles would score 0 or 1 of 2.

**Fix.** This is not an instruction to rewrite any locked H2 title (PLAN.md section 5.3 reserves
that to the update protocol, and the titles in question are not wrong, only descriptive). The
lower-cost fix is to note, once, in section 0 or in this critique's own record, that the action-
title dimension is being applied selectively: argument-carrying sections (2, 3, 9, 12 to 16, 20,
21, 25) are held to the number-led standard and already meet it, while reference and navigation
sections (0, 10, 26, 27, 29) and the three landscape surveys (5, 6, 7, each of which is explicitly
scoped as survey depth, not an argued finding, per section 1 and the plan's own section 3.2) are a
different kind of section and are reasonably exempt from the same rule a slide headline follows.

**Why it matters.** Without that stated exemption, a critic or a judge applying the rubric
mechanically would mark the document down on Dimension 4 for sections that were never trying to
be argument slides in the first place, which would be scoring the document against a rule it
never claimed to follow for those sections. Naming the exemption once removes the ambiguity
without touching a single title.
