## What the deck does well

1. **The contrarian recommendation is fully worked, not asserted.** S2's P3 panel states the obvious play
   (cash-per-tyre) fairly, in its own red-struck box, then the adjacent green box answers each of its four
   failure points with a specific number from the model (₹41.7 crore vs ₹22.0 crore, 91% deadweight). This is
   the comparison table the rubric's option-filter pass/fail item asks for, executed with real figures on
   both sides rather than a one-sided strawman.
2. **Every scenario claim is bear/base/bull, never a point estimate.** The moneyshot card, the P7 budget
   columns, and all eight rows of A4's scenario table carry three values. A3's tornado adds the sophistication
   signal the rubric's Dimension 3 top band asks for (which assumption swings break-even most, not just that
   it has a range).
