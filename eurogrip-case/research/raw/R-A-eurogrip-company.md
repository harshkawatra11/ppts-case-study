RA02 (NSE filing, 2024) gives "Vellaripatti, Melur Taluk, Madurai-625122" as the registered office and manufacturing unit; RA100 (multiple 2024 NSE/BSE compliance filings) gives the registered office as "TVS Building, 7-B, West Veli Street, Madurai 625001." Ruling: treat Vellaripatti as a manufacturing-plant address and West Veli Street as the company's registered office; both are real TVS Srichakra addresses for different purposes, not a factual conflict once disambiguated. Also appended to raw/conflicts.md.
- C-RA2: Employee count. A WebSearch snippet of the Reveliolabs page (RA07) gives 1,743 employees in 2025; a direct fetch of the same page's own annual chart (RA77) shows approximately 2,690 for 2025, and the page's narrative text and FAQ disagree with its own chart on multiple years (2,723 vs 2,799 for 2023; 2,791 vs 2,690 for 2025). Ruling: this is an internally inconsistent third-party aggregator, not an authoritative company disclosure; present employee count in the document as an unreconciled range (roughly 1,700 to 2,800), not a single figure, and flag Reveliolabs as the weakest-tier source in this lane. Also appended to raw/conflicts.md.
- C-RA3: CSK sponsorship deal value. One exchange4media search-result snippet (RA38) is headlined "Did CSK bag Rs 23 crore sponsorship deal with TVS Srichakra?"; other press coverage reports a figure of ₹100 crore; the Tyrepress announcement confirmed directly (RA37/RA101) states no deal value at all. Ruling: do not use either ₹23 crore or ₹100 crore in the document as a confirmed deal value; both are unconfirmed press figures and the only directly-opened primary-adjacent source is silent on value. Also appended to raw/conflicts.md.
- C-RA4: FY23/FY22 revenue and PAT, CRISIL vs Screener.in. CRISIL's 22 Apr 2024 rationale (RA97) gives FY2023 revenue ₹2,994 crore / PAT ₹78 crore and FY2022 revenue ₹2,549 crore / PAT ₹43 crore; Screener.in's consolidated aggregator table (RA12/RA14) gives FY23 revenue ₹2,985 crore / PAT ₹78 crore and FY22 revenue ₹2,543 crore / PAT ₹43 crore. PAT matches exactly in both years; revenue differs by ₹9 crore (FY23) and ₹6 crore (FY22), consistent with a standalone-vs-consolidated or revenue-vs-total-income definitional gap rather than a genuine error. Ruling: use the Screener.in consolidated figures (RA12) as the document's headline revenue series since they span FY21-FY26 continuously; treat the CRISIL figures as a close cross-confirmation, not a correction. Also appended to raw/conflicts.md.
