## Contradictions Found

1. **Location name: "Institute" vs. "Nitro," West Virginia.**
   - Most news outlets (Wikipedia, Mountain State Spotlight, West Virginia Watch, WV
     MetroNews initial headline, WCHS, WVPB, PBS NewsHour, ABC News, Fortune, The Hill,
     Powell & Majestro, Pritzker Law) describe the incident as occurring in **Institute,
     WV**.
   - The CSB's own official press release states the facility is "Catalyst Refiners, Inc.
     facility in **Nitro, West Virginia**." ([CSB](https://www.csb.gov/us-chemical-safety-board-opens-investigation-into-fatal-chemical-incident-at-catalyst-refiners-facility-in-west-virginia-/))
   - **Ames Goldsmith's own company statement** also says "Nitro, West Virginia": "an
     industrial accident at our Catalyst Refiners plant in Nitro, West Virginia" ([WV
     MetroNews](https://wvmetronews.com/2026/04/22/ames-goldsmith-confirms-fatal-tragedy-in-kanawha-county-plant-occurred-during-decommissioning-work/)).
   - Other sources split the difference by describing the plant as sitting directly on the
     **Institute/Nitro border**, or give a street address (1580 1st Avenue South) which
     multiple directories (Yelp, IndustryNet, Marshall University B2B WV listing, WV
     Division of Economic Development) list under a **Nitro, WV 25143** mailing address,
     while news framing defaults to "Institute" — likely because Institute is the more
     recognizable Kanawha Valley place name near WV State University. ([Powell &
     Majestro](https://www.powellmajestro.com/institute-wv-nitric-acid-leak-at-ames-goldsmith-catalyst-refiners-legal-options-for-affected-residents/);
     [Marshall University B2B WV](https://www.mfg.marshall.edu/industry/b2bwv/member-companies/ames-goldmsith-catalyst-refiners/);
     [WV MetroNews 4/22 vs. AOL syndication both use "border of Nitro and
     Institute"](https://www.aol.com/articles/air-monitoring-investigation-launched-chemical-175109000.html))
   - **Conclusion for dossier purposes: log as unresolved — this is a genuine,
     multi-source naming discrepancy, not a single outlier error.** Do not silently pick
     one; both "Institute" (predominant news usage) and "Nitro" (CSB + company's own
     statement, and the registered street address) should be carried in the dossier.

2. **Exact time of release: 9:30 a.m. (approx./consensus) vs. 9:32 a.m. (unconfirmed) vs.
   9:38 a.m. (911 call) vs. 9:46 a.m. (Wikipedia-stated).**
   - "Approximately 9:30 a.m." is the most commonly repeated figure (The Hill, Powell &
     Majestro, WV MetroNews, Fortune, WV MetroNews 5/3 article).
   - Wikipedia's infobox specifically states **9:46 a.m. EST** as the release time — this
     is later than the ~9:30 a.m. figure used everywhere else, and appears to conflate the
     release time with the EOC-activation time (9:46–9:50 a.m., per WV MetroNews/Pritzker
     Law).
   - **9:32 a.m., as flagged in the task brief, was not located in any fetched or
     search-indexed source in this pass** — logged as unconfirmed/not found rather than
     silently dropped. It is plausible this figure exists in a source not surfaced by the
     searches run (e.g., a specific TV-affiliate ticker or the CSB's eventual full
     investigation report, which had not yet published as of the most recent sources
     found).
   - 9:38 a.m. is consistently cited as the first 911 call time (a downstream event, not
     the release itself).

3. **Injured/hospitalized headcount: at least five different aggregate figures in
   circulation** — 19, 21, 22, ~30, and "30+ sought care" — see the Casualties table above
   for the full breakdown and sourcing of each variant. No single source reconciles all of
   these; they appear to reflect different measurement points (on-scene triage vs.
   hospital-transported vs. hospital-treated-total vs. a rounded/aggregated wire-style
   figure). The Powell & Majestro figure of "seven people transported" is a clear outlier
   likely describing a subset (e.g., only ambulance-agency-confirmed transports at time of
   its publication) rather than the full casualty count.

4. **Plant opening/founding year.** The task brief states "search says 1999" for when this
   specific plant opened; **no source fetched or indexed in this research pass states
   1999.** What was found instead is a separate, seemingly parallel licensing history for
