## Summary: directly comparable vs. general-industry-proxy figures

**Directly comparable (same hazard class, same year, or same venue):**
- Woodland Pulp LLC OSHA penalty ($794,456, willful H2S-alarm/PPE failures, 2026) — closest analog for what
  an OSHA outcome against Ames Goldsmith could look like.
- Freedom Industries ($151M) and DuPont C8 ($670.7M) settlements — same Kanawha Valley/WV chemical-industry
  litigation venue, establishing jury-pool/venue behavior.
- OSHA maximum penalty schedule ($16,550 serious / $165,514 willful-repeat / $16,550-per-day abatement) —
  directly applicable statutory ceiling for any future Ames Goldsmith citation.
- EPCRA Tier II penalty range ($23,331–$174,985/violation/day) — directly applicable if EPA pursues the
  confirmed Tier II omission of Bonderite M2000A.

**General industry benchmarks used as proxies only (not incident-specific):**
- NSC cost-per-death ($1.54M) / cost-per-injury ($48,000) — national all-industry averages.
- Gas-detector and portable-monitor unit prices — general industrial market prices, not a facility-specific
  quote.
- Refinery turnaround delay costs ($1M+/day, $5M–$100M+ total) — major-refinery-scale benchmarks, likely
  overstated relative to Ames Goldsmith's smaller WV facility; label explicitly as an illustrative proxy if
  used at all.
- Channelview/BWC Terminals OSHA fines (>$3.5M) — different chemical and different incident phase
  (post-spill cleanup, not the initiating event); useful only as an order-of-magnitude proxy for multi-party
  OSHA exposure.
