# Shared Facts Baseline — Ames Goldsmith / Catalyst Refiners Incident

Every chapter-writing agent MUST use these figures consistently. Where sources conflict, carry the
conflict forward explicitly (do not silently pick one) — but use this document's framing of the
conflict, don't re-litigate it differently in each chapter.

## Core facts
- **Date:** April 22, 2026.
- **Location:** most national/local news (Mountain State Spotlight, WV MetroNews, NPR, PBS, NBC) say
  **Institute, WV**; the CSB's own press release and Ames Goldsmith's own company statement say
  **Nitro, WV**; the registered street address (1580 1st Avenue South) resolves to Nitro, WV 25143 in
  business directories. Present as: "Institute (per most press coverage) / Nitro (per CSB and the
  company's own statement), Kanawha County, West Virginia" on first use per chapter, then whichever is
  natural — always flag on first use.
- **Facility:** Catalyst Refiners, Inc., owned by Ames Goldsmith Corporation (HQ South Glens Falls, NY;
  corporate lineage traces to Ames Chemical Co., founded 1860, silver reclamation from photographic
  waste; acquired by GAF in the 1960s; re-formed as Ames Goldsmith 1979). WV site refines silver and
  ethylene-oxide (EO) catalysts — three N. American refining plants combined >1M lb/month capacity,
  99.9% Ag purity guarantee, electrolytic (Moebius-type) refining. Plant was in **permanent
  decommissioning**, scheduled to close **June 2026**. A 1999 opening date is reported by news but
  could not be independently corroborated; a separate, distinct WV business-license history shows a
  1997 license, revoked 2015, reopened under the current name in 2018 — log both, don't merge them.
- **Release time:** **CORRECTED per Chapter 03's direct read of CSB primary materials — CSB's own press
  release/case page states 9:32 a.m. as the incident time.** Use 9:32 a.m. (CSB, primary source) as the
  authoritative release time; retain "~9:30 a.m." as general press-consensus rounding; flag 9:46 a.m.
  (Wikipedia infobox) as likely EOC-activation time, not release time; first 911 call logged at 9:38 a.m.
  Do NOT describe 9:32 a.m. as "unconfirmed" in any chapter — it is CSB-sourced.
- **Mechanism:** nitric acid (HNO3) + Bonderite M2000A (a Henkel industrial cleaning product; exact
  formulation of "M2000A" specifically is not publicly disclosed — a similarly-named product
  "BONDERITE M-AD 2000A," an alkaline corrosion-inhibited cleaner, was located but the exact identity
  match to the product named in press coverage is unconfirmed) were introduced into a legacy vessel
  holding "silver mud" — Ag2S-bearing residue from spent Ag/EO-catalyst refining. This triggered a
  violent reaction releasing **hydrogen sulfide (H2S) — confirmed across essentially all open
  reporting**. **Nitrogen dioxide (NO2) is NOT independently confirmed as a released agent in any
  open-source reporting found** — treat NO2 only as a chemically expected co-product per the reaction
  chemistry (chapter 04), explicitly labeled [INFERRED], never presented on the same evidentiary
  footing as the [VERIFIED] H2S release.
- **Casualties:** 2 fatalities (uncontested); names not publicly identified as of 2026-08-02.
  Hospitalized/treated counts vary by source with **at least five variants: 19, 21, 22, ~30, "30+"**
  — CSB's own case page separately cites an unreconciled "4 additional injuries" figure — present as
  a range/table, not a single number. **Seven first responders/EMS personnel among the injured is
  well-corroborated across multiple sources.** Hospitals treating victims: CAMC Charleston, WVU
  Medicine Thomas Memorial. Chapter 03 (`03-incident-reconstruction.md`) is the authoritative source
  for this framing — defer to it if any other chapter drafted before this correction used different
  wording.
- **Shelter-in-place:** ordered for Kanawha County schools and West Virginia State University,
  roughly a 1-mile radius per legal-summary secondary source; later lifted same day/next day per news.

## Regulatory record
- **CSB:** opened a formal investigation **April 23, 2026** (Chairperson Steve Owens, Board Member
  Sylvia Johnson quoted). Dedicated case page confirms status "ongoing" — **no final report, no
  case/investigation number, no target date published**. Any causal statement not attributed to CSB
  is OUR reconstruction — always label it as such.
- **OSHA:** opened an inspection with the standard ~6-month statutory deadline (due ~Oct 22, 2026;
  the 2026 fatality-inspection IMIS record is not yet public). Prior OSHA history at this
  establishment (IMIS record 1359924.015, directly fetched): a **2018 citation** (29 CFR
  1910.178(l)(4.iii), powered-industrial-truck operator evaluation, **$0 penalty**, opened
  Nov 14 2018, closed Sept 9 2019); a **2007 DOL inspection** (8 violations, 5 serious) following a
  hydrogen-peroxide/silver-oxide vat incident that hospitalized 4 workers; a **2015 corporate license
  revocation**, reopened under the current business name in 2018 (distinct from the 1999 opening
  claim — log both).
- **2013 tank leak:** nitric acid spill, injured two people. Volume reported inconsistently — press
  shorthand says "50 gallons"; a 2013 WVDEP inspection account (secondary source) cites ~100 gal in
  the vessel with ~75 gal spilled. Flag the discrepancy, don't pick one.
- **EPA/TRI:** facility reports nitric acid, silver, and silver compounds; 2020 on-site air releases
  of nitric acid were **220 lb** (from a directly-fetched TRI Explorer facility profile). The
  widely-repeated "1,310 lb nitric acid released 2014–2024" figure traces to **Wikipedia only** and
  could not be independently confirmed against a primary multi-year TRI query — flag as single-source.
  **No RMP (Risk Management Plan) filing was found either way** — this is an unresolved research gap,
  NOT evidence of "no RMP."
- **WVDEP:** ran joint post-incident air monitoring with EPA (11–12 monitors); consistently reported
  **non-detect / no offsite H2S detected** through at least April 29, 2026 (spokesman Terry Fletcher
  quoted April 24).
- **Tier II / EPCRA hazard communication gap (high-value, confirmed via search-synthesis, WV
  Gazette-Mail, published May 7, 2026):** Bonderite M2000A ("M-2000A") was **not listed on Catalyst
  Refiners' federally required EPCRA Tier II Emergency and Hazardous Chemical Inventory form**.
  Emergency responders reportedly located the product's data sheet via a **~20-minute Google search
  during the active response**, rather than from facility hazard documentation. This is a genuine,
  well-sourced hazard-communication root cause — use it prominently in the root-cause and regulatory
  chapters. (Flagged in `_research/lane7-gapfill.md` as search-engine-synthesized, not a verbatim
  primary-source read — direct fetch of the article returned HTTP 429.)
- **Officials/spokespeople quoted across sources:** WV Governor Morrisey; Ames Goldsmith President
  Frank Barber; WVDEP spokesman Terry Fletcher; CSB Chairperson Steve Owens; CSB Board Member
  Sylvia Johnson.

## Strongest comparative precedents (see `lane6-precedent-tech.md` for full detail)
- **CSB Safety Bulletin No. 2003-03-B, "Sodium Hydrosulfide: Preventing Harm" (2004)** — 45 tracked
  NaHS/acid-sulfide H2S incidents (1971–2004), 32 deaths, 176 injuries. Closest generic chemistry
  precedent (acid + sulfide → H2S).
- **CSB Investigation Report No. 2002-01-I-AL, Georgia-Pacific Naheola Mill (2002)** — closest
  structural analog: an unreviewed connection between an acid stream and a sulfide-bearing stream,
  no MOC/hazard review performed, contractors killed near a manway.

## Epistemic tiers — use these tags inline, every chapter
- **[VERIFIED]** — ≥2 independent sources, or one primary source (CSB, OSHA, EPA, WVDEP, direct company
  statement). Cite with a local `[S-nn]` marker resolved in that chapter's own source list at the
  bottom of the file.
- **[VERIFIED, search-synthesis]** — confirmed via search-engine result synthesis where a direct fetch
  of the primary article failed (403/429/timeout) — used for the Tier II/M2000A finding and similar.
  Treat as reliable but flag the access limitation once, in that chapter's source list.
- **[INFERRED]** — engineering/logical reconstruction from verified facts. Show the reasoning chain.
- **[HYPOTHESIS]** — plausible but unconfirmed; list the alternative(s) too.

Never state a CSB "cause" — there is no final report. Never invent operator names, dialogue, internal
permits, or documents. Every quantitative claim needs a tag and a citation.
