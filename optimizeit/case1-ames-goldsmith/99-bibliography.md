# Master Bibliography — Ames Goldsmith / Catalyst Refiners Dossier

Consolidated, deduplicated, globally-numbered bibliography compiled from the chapter-local
`## Sources` sections of Chapters 02–14. Each chapter used locally-numbered `[S-nn]` tags;
this document assigns clean global `[G-nnn]` numbers and records which chapter(s) cited each
source. This is a compilation task only — no new research was performed and no URLs were
invented. Where a chapter's source entry was itself vague (e.g. "general professional
knowledge, not re-fetched"), that caveat is preserved verbatim rather than sharpened.

---

## Access-limitation note (read this before weighting any source below)

Several outlets returned HTTP errors on direct fetch during the underlying research and were
relied on via **search-engine synthesis** instead of a verified direct read of the full
article. These are flagged inline below with **[search-synthesis]**, and are, as a class,
weaker evidence than directly-fetched sources — treat their content as "reported to say," not
as independently verified quotation. The pattern observed:

- **HTTP 403 (blocked outright):** NBC News, West Virginia Watch, HAZMAT Management, WOWK
  (Thomas Memorial Hospital "22 patients" figure).
- **HTTP 429 (rate-limited):** WV Gazette-Mail ("Officials were in the dark on key chemical...",
  May 7, 2026 — this is the single most load-bearing search-synthesis source in the dossier,
  cited by 6 of 13 chapters), WCHS-TV follow-up fetch attempt.
- **Headline/summary confirmed via search only, full text not retrieved:** PMCF transactions
  page (S-04 in Ch.02), WOWK "air samples still clear" (Ch.03 S-32).

Primary-source government documents (CSB press release and case page, OSHA IMIS record,
EPA TRI Explorer) were **directly fetched** and are the strongest-tier evidence in the dossier.
Internal dossier cross-references (`FACTS-BASELINE.md`, other chapters) are treated as
tertiary/organizational, not independent evidence.
