## Useful contextual conclusions

Indian refurbished demand can grow while new-device shipments weaken, but that observation does not establish Tikau's potential demand uplift. Counterpoint's H1 2026 release directly supports the case's two market growth rates. It also highlights an informal majority, so organized platforms are only part of the category. [B01 to B05]

Market labels need care. IDC's India used-device coverage includes both second-hand and refurbished phones through organized refurbishers and explicitly excludes peer-to-peer sales. Counterpoint's global release separately describes devices sold as-is with no technical work. Refurbished, pre-owned, secondary and repaired are not interchangeable measures of a consistently defined market. [B11, B19]

Demand and supply are linked. Longer holding periods can restrict the supply of recent devices even as consumers seek cheaper premium phones. Historical national or global observations establish a possible market mechanism, not a Tikau capacity or sourcing assumption. [B07, B15, B22, B24]

Category trust involves repair information, authenticity, performance and warranty scope. The sources do not demonstrate that one promise produces a specified conversion gain, return reduction or willingness to pay among Tikau buyers. Vendor commitments illustrate how promises are packaged; they do not prove those commitments were delivered. [B08, B26 to B28, B30, B32 to B36]

## Remaining gaps

The requested GSMA trust-gap statistic needs direct access to the original article or report, including its population, question wording and fieldwork. Other GSMA original report and newsroom URLs also returned access errors. The original Counterpoint December 2024 unorganized-market report has a readable abstract but subscription-only detail, so its alleged 77% split was not promoted from secondary search results into this register. Flipkart's original Reset for Business PDF likewise returned an access error and was not used as verified evidence.

No national incidence rate for defects in refurbished smartphones was verified. NCH totals do not supply a denominator or isolate new from refurbished products. No causal Indian evidence was found here showing that a battery guarantee, longer warranty or disclosure package delivers the case lever effects. Those effects remain solely case inputs.
