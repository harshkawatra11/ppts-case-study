## Verification method and remaining gaps

Full official documents or substantive official pages were opened, with section-level reads for the claims. Search snippets and secondary summaries were used only for discovery. Every row identifies the actual primary source. Original rules are not represented as consolidated legal texts. No legal interpretation is offered as a factual finding of Tikau's actual obligations without assessing scope.

The remaining boundaries are material:

- No full IS 19000 text was inspected. Do not attribute detailed incentive, review-moderation or buyer-only clauses to it using these sources.
- No complete IS 16046 standard was inspected. The BIS listing verifies titles and listed test headings only. It does not prove an 85% health threshold or test equivalence.
- No actual Tikau lab accreditation, product certification, waste registration, record, device test, police record or erasure log was provided.
- E-waste and battery-waste amendments after the original instruments were not exhaustively consolidated. The lane supports the cited base-rule and SOP propositions with explicit scope caveats, not a complete current compliance opinion.
- No new general Indian ban on every use of the words like new, or a universal 85% refurbished-phone health threshold, was established. Those presentation decisions are governed by the case.
- The most recent inspected official voluntary-status statement for IS 19000 is March 2026. It is not proof that no later notification could exist.
- No assertion is made that a clean IMEI result proves lawful ownership, activation-lock release, authenticity of every part or successful data sanitisation.
- Future amendments should be rechecked before any real commercial launch; no hypothetical obligation or external cost has been added to the case model.
