## How to use the evidence

Battery reports should identify the handset model, operating software, measurement source and test date. Record unavailable or unverified readings explicitly. A capacity estimate cannot establish precise runtime or remaining lifespan. Cycle recommendations differ across devices and cannot justify one universal replacement threshold.

Keep cosmetic condition, functional inspection, battery measurement, repaired-parts provenance, activation readiness and erasure as separate evidence fields. These are proposed report controls, not claims that Tikau has implemented or tested them. A compatible replacement can work without being brand made. Exhibit 5 prohibits the genuine-parts claim for Tikau's compatible batteries regardless of any manufacturer's broader repair terminology.

A sanitization record should connect the physical unit to the procedure, software version, completion result and acceptance decision. Describe a certificate as Tikau's record of the action and review. Do not suggest that NIST certified the phone, tool or refurbisher. Registry checks, lock checks and functional tests answer different questions and need their own dated results.
