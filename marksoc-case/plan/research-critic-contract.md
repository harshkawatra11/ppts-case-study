# Research integration review contract

Use the case-deck critique rubric adapted to this marketing and operating-turnaround case. Do not apply M&A valuation or financing requirements. Review all numbered sections and appendices, with the frozen case and model as authority. Report the five most consequential issues with exact section, claim, consequence and repair. State whether each is critical, major or editorial. A corrected model result cannot be averaged with an incorrect reading.

Check all five questions, the four board tests, the decision table and its required below-table items. Check that price-led, warranty-led, trade-in and combined alternatives receive fair treatment. The selection objective is the team's highest unrounded Q1 EBITDA among conditionally feasible plans, not an explicit objective imposed by the case.

Specifically inspect the small Q1 disadvantage, joint bear loss, ordered capacity rationing, conditional compliance proxy, exact price-sensitive commission, coupon cost layer, and the distinction between April ongoing benefit and setup recovery. Check that weekly warnings, cohort denominators and failure boundaries are labelled correctly. Segment weights, risk ratings and proposed validation rules are judgement, not observed results.

Review source conflicts and scope: market periods and denominators, final versus draft legal instruments, staged commencement, voluntary standards, vendor promises versus outcomes, device-specific technical caveats and the lack of new primary research. Outside research must not enter assessed model inputs or slide statistics. Verify at least sixty sources are substantively used and source-specific synthesis remains within permitted limits.

The humanizer review must identify patterns, rewrite awkward prose and compare numeric meaning before and after. A banned-word scan alone is insufficient. Flag repeated argument, inflated claims, staged openings, dramatic closers, unsupported contrast, missing subjects and lost qualifications. Preserve precise distinctions such as phones returned versus customers dissatisfied.

A table counts only when it contributes a distinct comparison, derivation, control or decision. Repeated model tables, boilerplate grids and wording changes around the same content do not establish analytical depth. Review the authored and generated word counts separately.

Return a clear PASS or FAIL and exact unresolved issues. The report cannot pass with a critical factual or mathematical error, missing section, invented primary evidence, context leakage or defective rendering. Geometry and citation diagnostics supplement human review; they do not prove semantic correctness.
