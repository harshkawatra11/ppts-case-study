## Gaps

- No primary SEBI circular, FAQ or informal guidance was found that directly answers whether a rule-built, no-default, objective-criteria neutral ETF list (the salary-day lever's mechanism) falls inside or outside the stock-broker incidental-advice carve-out (D05). This is the single most consequential open question for the lever's legality and should be escalated for a formal SEBI informal-guidance request before build, not assumed resolved by analogy.
- The primary NSE circular text for NSE/INSP/64718 (25 Oct 2024) and NSE/INSP/63425 (14 Aug 2024) was not retrieved directly; all facts on these two circulars rest on secondary or broker-compliance-document mirrors (D26, D29, D30).
- The exact current wording of the standard NSE/BSE advertisement-code risk line was not retrieved verbatim from a primary circular (D21); do not quote a risk-line wording in customer copy without pulling the current NSE/BSE PDF directly.
- SEBI's video-IPV permission circular (commonly cited 2020) was not independently confirmed with a primary citation (D35).
- The TRAI DLT scrubbing suffix and biometric-authentication claims (D73) rest on a single vendor page and are unconfirmed.
- No row answers the research-analyst "mass research call" question directly as a distinct regulated category (D11); this term appears to be case-vocabulary (C-PB3), not a defined regulatory term.

## Conflicts

- **RBI 2027 amendment effective date.** PLAN.md's planning-time note describes "RBI directions on banks referring group products and the 2027 conduct amendment" without a specific date; this verification found the RBI Commercial Banks, Responsible Business Conduct (Second Amendment) Directions 2026, issued 15 June 2026, taking effect 1 January 2027 (D66), while an earlier February 2026 draft of the same package had proposed an effective date of 1 July 2026 (per the Medianama summary, D67, and the ksandk.com account of the draft stage). The final 1 January 2027 date (D66) should be treated as the operative one; the 1 July 2026 figure is draft-stage only.
- **SEBI common advertisement code reporting window.** The June 2026 consultation paper proposed post-issuance reporting within 24 hours (D23); a later September 2026 SEBI board memorandum proposes 3 working days instead (D24). Treat 3 working days as the more current figure, since it postdates the consultation, but note neither may be the final rule until SEBI issues the actual amendment.
- **SEBI FY26 F&O study trader-count base.** One secondary summary gives active individual F&O traders falling from 98.1 lakh (FY25) to 78.6 lakh (FY26); another gives 106.2 lakh to 87.5 lakh for what appears to be the same metric (D48). The loss-rate figures (87.7%, under-30 88.5%) are consistent across sources and are the figures this plan should keep using; the base trader counts are not reconciled and should not be quoted without a primary SEBI citation.
- **SEBI digital gold caution: recommended alternatives.** SEBI's own press release lists gold ETFs, exchange-traded commodity derivatives and Electronic Gold Receipts as SEBI-regulated alternatives (D56); one secondary summary (Drishti IAS) additionally lists Sovereign Gold Bonds, which are an RBI/government debt instrument, not a SEBI-regulated product. Treat the SGB inclusion as a probable secondary-source error; do not cite SGBs as a SEBI-recommended alternative in deck copy.
- **NSE inducement circular status.** PLAN.md's planning-time note cites NSE/INSP/63425 (14 Aug 2024) as a current source of inducement rules; this verification found NSE placed that circular in abeyance from 24 January 2025 (NSE Circular 06/2025, D26) pending an industry-forum proposal, while also telling members to keep noting its contents (D27). The circular's legal force between abeyance and any resolution of the Brokers' ISF proposal is unclear from available sources; flag for the writers that citing NSE/INSP/63425 as settled current law may be incorrect, and the ONL inducement argument in section 3.3 should rest on the NSE advertisement code and the general SEBI/NSE anti-inducement principle rather than solely on this circular.
