## Conflicts

- Dhanteras 2026 date: consensus (Shubh Panchang, SmartPuja, Dekho Panchang, Universal Time Date, Hindutone/Drik Panchang) is Friday, 6 November 2026 (G01, G05); one source (chartlocal) gives 5 November, with Diwali on 10 November and Bhai Dooj on 14 November (G04). The chartlocal figures are treated as unreliable because no other source corroborates them and the Bhai Dooj date would be inconsistent with the standard five-day festival spacing.
- 2026 Muhurat Trading date: the exchange-circular-sourced consensus (GoodReturns, TeamLease RegTech, Business Today, Kotak Neo) is Sunday, 8 November 2026 (G13); one BSE-side aggregator (Angel One BSE holidays page) instead lists the session as Wednesday, 21 October 2026, "timings to be notified later" (G09). The 21 October date appears to be a stale or miscopied entry, since it predates even Dhanteras (6 November) and conflicts with every other source, including Angel One's own NSE holiday page.
- Dhanteras 2025 gold-sales volume: the All India Gem and Jewellery Domestic Council is quoted both as saying volumes fell 10-15% year on year (G22) and, in a later estimate for the same festival, that volumes were "similar to last year" at an estimated 50-60 tonnes worth nearly Rs 85,000 crore (G23). Both figures are kept and flagged, since the gap likely reflects pre-festival versus post-festival estimates from the same body, not a resolved correction.
- Makar Sankranti and Pongal 2027: the main-day consensus (Drik Panchang, Calendarific) is 15 January 2027, with the solar transit occurring late on 14 January (G38, G39); Calendar Labs instead lists 14 January for Pongal and Magh Bihu (G40). The DoPT central gazetted list (G41) does not resolve this, since it does not carry either festival as a gazetted date.
- BSE 2026 weekday holiday dates: ICICI Direct/mStock give Holi as 3 March, Ram Navami as 26 March and Bakrid as 28 May (G10); BankBazaar instead gives Holi as 4 March, Ram Navami as 27 March and Bakrid as 27 May (G11).
