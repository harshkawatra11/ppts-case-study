## Conflicts

1. Press coverage of the FINRA action (H31) gives "$70 million" as the total Robinhood penalty (a $57 million fine plus about $12.6 million restitution). A separate, earlier SEC penalty against Robinhood of $65 million (from 2020, for misleading customers about payment for order flow) is sometimes mentioned in the same articles; writers should not conflate the 2020 SEC $65 million figure with the 2021 FINRA $70 million figure, as they are two different regulators' actions in two different years.
2. The Massachusetts "gamification" settlement (H34 to H36) was filed 18 January 2024, not 2022; the underlying complaint dates to December 2020, with a further complaint in 2021. Any reference to "the Massachusetts gamification settlement" without a year should use 2024 as the settlement date and 2020/2021 as the original complaint dates.
3. Groww's own framing of its product history is not fully consistent across sources: one secondary account (H02) frames mutual funds as the sole original product with stock trading added later as a cross-sell, while another (H03) frames the mutual-fund-first pattern as a newer (post-2023) behavioral shift among a different cohort of customers responding to market conditions, not a feature of the platform's original design. Both can be true at different points in Groww's history; writers should specify which period (2017 to 2020 versus post-2023) a claim refers to.
4. Two different figures for Kotak 811-to-trading cross-sell appear in search results without full agreement on period: H11 (57% of 811's contribution to Kotak Bank in Q4 FY22, by volume) is the only figure with a named source and a specific quarter; no comparable figure for a more recent period was found, so it should not be presented as a current-year statistic.
