- R-E-04: Firstsource's FY2025/FY2026E multiples came from a search summary of a broker page, not a direct read; treat as indicative only.
- R-E-06, R-E-07: both BPO precedent deals (TransWorks, CustomerAsset) are from the mid-2000s BPO consolidation wave, not 2025/2026; they are useful only as a pattern ("sub-scale BPO sold for a modest multiple when squeezed"), not as current pricing evidence. Say so explicitly if used.
- E3: no sourced benchmark for deal-closing time in India exists in this pass; the model's "close at month 5" assumption (A18) should be labelled a team assumption with no external anchor, not softened.
- E4: no specific Indian lender covenant-breach case was opened; the research document should state covenant mechanics (waiver/reset/recall) as general practice, not as a cited finding.
- R-E-13 is the team's own legal inference connecting E5 to Lane B's IBC Section 53 findings, not an independently sourced fact, and must be labelled as such in the research document per the plan's content-integrity rule 2.1.7 (legal statements hedged, counsel to confirm).
