source register in the plan (section 3) and to Appendix A of the research document. [snip] = snippet-only source.

| F-P01 | Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024: introduced in the Lok Sabha on 5 Feb 2024; in force from June 2024; covers UPSC, SSC, RRBs, IBPS, NTA and central ministries and departments | S16, S03 |
| F-P02 | 2024 Act penalties: unfair means 3 to 5 years and up to ₹10 lakh; service provider up to ₹1 Cr plus proportionate exam cost, and 4-year debarment; organised crime 5 to 10 years and at least ₹1 Cr | S16, S03 |

| F-P12 | PRS key issues: the rigid trial deadline conflicts with a 2002 Supreme Court ruling against outer limits for criminal trials; no consequence for missing investigation deadlines; existing fast-track special courts carry over 2.45 lakh pending cases (2025), with trials of 257 to 1,717 days, although they dispose of 9.51 cases a month against 3.26 in regular courts | S17 |
| F-P13 | State laws: Uttarakhand (2023) life imprisonment and up to ₹10 Cr; Jharkhand (2023) life and up to ₹10 Cr; Rajasthan (2022) up to 10 years and ₹10 Cr, later made stricter; Gujarat (2023) 5 to 10 years and ₹10 lakh to ₹1 Cr for organised leaks; Uttar Pradesh (2024) two years to life and ₹1 lakh to ₹1 Cr; Bihar (2024) 3 to 10 years and ₹10 lakh to ₹1 Cr | S21, S22, S23 |
| F-P14 | Radhakrishnan Committee: constituted 22 Jun 2024; chaired by Dr K. Radhakrishnan (former ISRO chair) with Randeep Guleria, Prof B.J. Rao, Prof Ramamurthy K, Pankaj Bansal and Prof Aditya Mittal; 23 full-day sittings; about 37,000 MyGov responses; report Oct 2024 with 101 recommendations. Key items: DIGI-EXAM identity (Aadhaar, biometrics, AI analytics); computer-assisted pen-and-paper testing (encrypted e-transmission, high-speed printing at centres, unique codes on each copy); multi-session testing for exams over two lakh candidates; multi-stage tests; at least 1,000 secure testing centres in government institutions; mobile test centres (about 150 seats) for remote areas; NTA to focus on entrance exams; ten verticals including information security and vigilance; a Director General of Additional Secretary rank; permanent staff; a steering committee for mission-mode delivery | S61, S62, S12 |
