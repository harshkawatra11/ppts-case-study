## Intent

A launch and demonstration video for Pukaar, an AI voice companion engineered for women's commute safety in India.
Pukaar replaces the common coping mechanism of faking a phone call with a genuine, low-latency, two-way Hinglish voice conversation where duress triggers are woven naturally into normal dialogue.

Tone: Grounded, analytical, authoritative, completely devoid of hype or filler adjectives. Clear engineering clarity and rigorous consulting-grade data citations.

Total runtime: 75 to 85 seconds (~1:15 to 1:25).
