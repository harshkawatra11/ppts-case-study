Land and land revenue
List II, Entry 18 (Land)
Land and land revenue is a State subject (List II, Entry 18 (Land)), but the selected authority, Department of Land Resources, is a Central authority. Under DoPT OM No. 10/2/2008-IR, a Central Public Information Officer is not required to transfer a State-subject application: it will be returned to the applicant, without a refund of the fee.
