ELIGIBLE

Sources disagree on some figures for this scheme.

✓ SECC 2011 deprivation category (via BPL/AAY ration card) or age 70 and above

Health cover up to Rs. 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary hospitalisation.

A free LPG connection with a security-deposit waiver, plus subsidised refills.
