## Conflicts

1. **MSME export share, FY25**: 45.79% (B01, SBI CEA Soumya Kanti Ghosh, dated variously FY24/May 2024/FY25 across outlets, see B02) versus 48.55% (B07, KNN India, explicitly dated FY25). Both describe "MSME share of exports" for the same period; no single PIB release URL was located to resolve which is the government's own headline figure.
2. **Exporting MSME count for 2024-25**: 1,73,350 (B03, sourced to Lok Sabha Unstarred Question 2786) versus 1,76,315 (B04, SBI-sourced secondary reporting on the same underlying period). The baseline for 2020-21 (52,849) is consistent across both.
3. **Udyam women-owned MSME share**: 20.5% (B12, Udyam Registration Portal only, July 2020 to January 2024) versus 40% (B13, Udyam Registration Portal plus Udyam Assist Platform, as of 28 November 2024) versus 38% (B14, 2026 secondary compilation, scope unclear). These may not be a true conflict since B12 and B13 cover different platform scopes and dates, but no single source reconciles all three; record all three with their scope caveats.
4. **GTRI e-commerce export baseline**: about $2 billion (B18, GTRI's own March 2023 report) versus about $5 billion (B19, a 2024 GTRI-linked update), both set against the same $350 billion by 2030 target. The $5 billion figure also roughly matches the separate EY-ASSOCHAM FY23 estimate of $4-5 billion (B15), which may be the true source of the discrepancy (GTRI's 2023 report may have used an earlier or narrower baseline than its 2024 update).
5. **Moradabad brassware export value**: approximately ₹2,200 crore (older government-linked figure) versus approximately ₹4,500 crore (more recent press estimate) for the same cluster's annual exports (B44). GI tag year is also reported as 2013 (most sources) versus 2023 (one outlier source, B44).
6. **Varanasi/Banarasi silk weaver counts**: approximately 26,779 district handloom weavers (ODOP project document, B47) versus approximately 200,000 Banarasi sari weavers specifically (Down To Earth, citing weavers' associations) versus "two million odd weavers" across the wider GI area (Millennium Post). These likely reflect different geographic and craft-type scopes (district handloom census vs. sari-specific vs. wider GI region) but no source reconciles them; record all three with scope caveats.
