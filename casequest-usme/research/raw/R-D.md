## Gaps

1. No source in this pass gave the RBI reference rate for the exact access date, 28 October 2026 (A01). The nearest dated prints found are 18 May 2026 (96.2948) and a 22 June 2026 close (94.68); neither is usable as the 28 October 2026 figure.
2. The 2026 Post Office (Amendment) Regulations' actual Table VIII rate figures (per-country, per weight slab) for the USA, UK, UAE and the EU were not located; only the fact of the substitution and its gazette reference were found.
3. No FedEx, Aramex or India Post calculator screenshot with a live 0.5 kg quote and today's date was obtainable, since WebFetch could not reach any of these domains in this session.
4. No primary CBIC, DGFT, DoP or RBI page could be opened directly; every row above rests on a secondary search snippet, which is why the lane's status column leans heavily on "VERIFIED (search only)" and "UNVERIFIABLE" rather than a WebFetch-confirmed VERIFIED.
5. No published cluster-level or collective freight tender for Indian exporter clusters was found (D39); A28's "pooled rate card" lever currently has no documented real-world precedent in India beyond the LIFT reimbursement scheme and the Aramex-FIEO MoU.

## Conflicts

1. ITPS weight ceiling to the USA: a 2025 rate table caps it at 2 kg, while a carrier-aggregator blog says it was raised to 5 kg (D08). Neither source is the 2026 Table VIII text itself.
2. DHL Express India-to-USA 0.5 kg rate: the same aggregator (Clickpost) gives ₹2,432 in a May 2025 table and ₹1,800 to ₹2,000 in a separate 2026-dated guide (D15 vs D16).
3. Royal Mail International Tracked Small Parcel 2 kg price: £5.45 in one reseller table vs £3.99 to £5.09 in another reseller's January 2026 table, with neither confirmed against Royal Mail's own Business Price Guide (D30).
4. USPS January 2026 international rate increase: 5.9% average per a Federal Register-tracking site (D26) vs roughly 4% to 8% (First-Class Package International specifically near 8%) in shipping-industry blogs (D27).
5. Amazon FBA small-standard fulfilment fee for January 2026: ranges quoted as $2.41-$3.34, $2.43-$4.22 and "approximately $3.07-$3.31" across three vendor blogs, for what is described as the same size/weight band (D31).
6. Amazon FBA Q4 standard storage fee: $2.60 per cubic foot in one source vs a separate claim of a cut to $2.25 (from $2.40) in another, both for the same October-to-December window (D32).
7. Amazon's 3.5% fuel/logistics surcharge effective date: 17 April 2026 in one source vs 15 April 2026 in another (D33).
8. RBI-linked rupee/dollar prints found in this pass span 88.68 (date unclear), 94.68 (22 June 2026) and 96.2948 (18 May 2026); these are not mutually consistent as a single trend line without the intervening daily data, which was not available through WebSearch snippets alone.
