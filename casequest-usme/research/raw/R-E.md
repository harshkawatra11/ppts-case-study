## Gaps

1. No source confirms the postal duty rate actually applied to India-origin parcels entering the US in October 2026 (today's access date). The only India-specific rate found (50% of declared FOB value, E16) is dated to the October 2025 resumption announcement; whether it was superseded by the ad valorem-only mandate of 28 February 2026 (E11) is unresolved (E19).
2. No primary CBP source (CSMS number or Federal Register notice) was opened directly for the ad valorem-only phase-out date of 28 February 2026; it rests on a secondary citation (policyrisk.com) of a CSMS bulletin.
3. The EU handling fee's amount and effective date are not fixed in any source found; the European Commission's own page (E21) does not mention a handling fee at all, only the EUR 3 duty itself.
4. No official UAE Federal Tax Authority or Dubai Customs page was found in any search snippet; all UAE threshold and VAT figures rest on commercial/vendor guides (E30 to E34).
5. No official HMRC notice confirming the 2028 abolition date and mechanism (E28) was found beyond a KPMG secondary summary.
6. Could not confirm whether any 2026 legislative change to the Australian low-value GST regime exists, or whether all located headlines describe the original 2017 to 2018 process (E38).

## Conflicts

1. US postal duty basis: flat specific duty ($80 to $200 per item, E10) versus ad valorem duty at the effective IEEPA rate (E10), versus a claimed flat 10% Section 122 tariff (E13, unverifiable) versus the 50% of FOB value specific to India reported at the October 2025 resumption (E16). The 28 February 2026 CBP notice (E11) says only ad valorem is now permitted, which is inconsistent with E13's claim of a flat 10% rate running through July 2026.
2. EO 14388 timeline: the Federal Register gives a 20 February 2026 signing date (E04); one secondary summary places its effect at 24 February 2026 (E05); a Troutman Pepper Locke article (18 August 2026) associates it with the 13 August 2026 CIT ruling (E07, E08), which does not match the February dates.
3. EU EUR 3 duty and IOSS: some sources say the duty applies to IOSS-registered sellers' consignments regardless; a Czech tax source (danovky.cz) says non-IOSS-registered entities instead remain on the ordinary Common Customs Tariff from 1 July 2026 (E23).
4. EU handling fee: reported variously as about EUR 2, starting "already from 1 November 2026" by one source, while another source says the level is "not fixed in this deal" and a third calls it still under negotiation (E24).
5. UAE VAT below the AED 1,000 threshold: some sources say 5% VAT still applies even though duty does not; another says neither duty nor VAT applies below the threshold (E31).
6. UAE land/sea threshold: one source claims AED 5,000 for land/sea imports versus AED 1,000 otherwise; uncorroborated elsewhere (E32).
