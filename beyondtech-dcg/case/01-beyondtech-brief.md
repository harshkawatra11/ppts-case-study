### 1. Company Background

BeyondTech helps Tier-1 B.Tech students crack the non-tech companies that visit their campus
placements, across consulting, product, analytics and business profiles. Instead of preparing for
each profile separately, students prepare for all of them under one course.

The company was started by its founder, Harsh, after he went through the "placement chaos" himself:
random guides, scattered tutorials and no clear path. In a sense, a student prepares for placements
their whole life. They work hard to get into a good college largely because they want a good
placement at the end of it. Yet placement prep is often treated as something that can be done in two
or three months, and it can't. Many students don't prepare in a structured way and get rejected in
some of the best interviews of their lives, opportunities that may come just once in their
undergraduate years.

BeyondTech exists to give students that structure for non-tech placements. Harsh is the founder and
lead mentor, with Subham as a feedback mentor.

### The offering

The flagship is a mentor-led "Non-Tech Placement Mastery Program". The brief describes it as an
11-week live and recorded bootcamp in four phases: **[B07]**

| Phase | Focus | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Consulting foundations | Problem solving | Guesstimates, case frameworks, profitability and market entry |
| 2. Analytics mastery | Data skills | SQL (basic, intermediate, advanced) and data cases |
| 3. Product strategy | Product thinking | Product cases, RCA, KPIs and design |
| 4. Personalized prep | Interview readiness | 1-on-1 mock interviews, off-campus guidance and off-campus referrals |

The site also lists company-specific previous-year questions (aptitude and interview) and weekly
mocks with leaderboards. Beyond campus placements, BeyondTech also provides off-campus guidance,
including off-campus referrals. **[B08]**

### Traction and positioning

- **Traction (2026 placement season):** at DTU, BeyondTech members have secured 50+ selections so
  far at firms such as Eternal, JP Morgan Chase, Bain, ZS, Everest and PwC, with an average package
  of 13-14 LPA and a highest of 47 LPA (Eternal). At NSUT, members were selected at Eternal, ZS,
  Bain, Prism and L.E.K. Consulting; at Thapar and BIT Mesra, at Bain, ZS and Deloitte.
- **Positioning today:** "One course for every non-tech company visiting your campus" **[B09]**
- **Growth engine:** word-of-mouth, community and founder-led mentorship, which is why DTU is so
  dense. **[B10]**

### 2. The Core Problem

BeyondTech has strong satisfaction and strong DTU brand recall, but its growth engine is informal and
tied to the founder's presence. Two bottlenecks stand out: **[B11]**

- **TAM limitation.** "Non-tech skills for engineers" is a narrow audience, and not every campus
  gets strong non-tech companies in its placements. To grow, BeyondTech needs to expand into more
  Tier-1 colleges across India. **[B12]**
- **Channel dependency.** Marketing relies entirely on word-of-mouth, BeyondTech's WhatsApp
  communities, and LinkedIn (the company page and the founder's personal profile). There is no
  proper marketing engine: no repeatable paid marketing and no structured ambassador flywheel, so
  growth can't be scaled or forecast. **[B13]**

### 3. Scope: Two Pillars

This case asks you to think about two things: how BeyondTech deepens its penetration at DTU, and how
it expands to Tier-1 colleges across India.

#### Vertical 1: Deepen DTU

DTU has a strong non-tech scene, and its non-tech placements are among the best of any engineering
campus. BeyondTech already has strong brand recall here, but new students still come in mostly
through referrals. How can BeyondTech grow its DTU student base significantly?

Ideas you may consider: **[B14]**
- **Low-cost distribution:** campus societies, department networks and the placement cell.
- **Paid channels:** targeted Instagram and LinkedIn ads, and campus influencers.
- **Urgency activations:** mock placement drives and case workshops that bring in high-intent
  students.

**Deliverable:** a GTM plan to increase BeyondTech's penetration at DTU, with a budget split between
organic and paid channels. **[B15]**

#### Vertical 2: Pan-India Tier-1 Expansion

Outside its core campuses (DTU, NSUT, Thapar and BIT Mesra), almost no one knows BeyondTech. Which
Tier-1 colleges should it launch at next, and how? **[B16]**

- **Pick 3-4 colleges:** choose Tier-1 colleges with a strong non-tech culture, compared on a simple
  scorecard (for example, non-tech companies visiting campus, active non-tech societies and
  placement timeline). Public data and clearly stated estimates are fine, as long as every pick is
  backed by numbers. **[B17]**
- **Plan the launch:** how BeyondTech enters each college without a local founder or word-of-mouth,
  for example through campus ambassadors, society tie-ups, digital ads and alumni success stories.
  **[B18]**

**Deliverable:** your 3-4 college picks with a one-line reason for each, and a GTM plan to launch at
them. **[B19]**

### 4. Deliverables and Scoring

A single PDF or PPTX of 8-12 slides, excluding title and appendix, covering:
- DTU GTM plan with a budget split between organic and paid.
- Pan-India expansion GTM with your 3-4 Tier-1 college picks, backed by data, and a plan to launch at
  them.
