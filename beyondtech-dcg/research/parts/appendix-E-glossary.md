## Appendix E. Glossary

Every acronym and abbreviated proper noun used across all six parts of this document, alphabetised. Where a term is also a company or society name, the expansion given is how that organisation itself states it, not an interpretation.

| Abbreviation | Explanation |
|---|---|
| A01 to A34 | This document's own assumption IDs (PLAN.md section 5.3, model_tables.md T16): a bear, base and bull figure with a stated basis, used wherever a number is modelled rather than measured |
| ACV | Annual contract value, a unit-economics term for the yearly revenue a single customer contract represents |
| AICTE | All India Council for Technical Education, the statutory regulator for technical education in India |
| APM | Associate Product Manager, an entry-level non-tech recruiter role profile |
| ASCI | Advertising Standards Council of India, the self-regulatory body whose education-sector and influencer-disclosure guidelines bound this plan's claims (section 23.2) |
| BCG | Boston Consulting Group, a named consulting-sector recruiter and casebook publisher referenced across the competition and demand research |
| BFC | Business, Finance & Consulting Club, NIT Tiruchirappalli's umbrella student body covering 180DC, ProfNITT and SIGMA |
| BHU | Banaras Hindu University, as in IIT (BHU) Varanasi, one of the plan's four contrast-set IITs |
| BITS | Birla Institute of Technology and Science, as in BITS Pilani (Pilani, Goa and Hyderabad campuses) |
| B.Tech | Bachelor of Technology, the undergraduate engineering degree this entire plan addresses |
| CAC | Customer acquisition cost, the cash spent per enrolment, the model's primary channel-efficiency metric (T3, T4, T6) |
| CAGR | Compound annual growth rate, a market-sizing metric named in this document's framework catalogue |
| CCPA | Central Consumer Protection Authority, the regulator behind the 2024 coaching-sector misleading-advertisement guidelines |
| CDGC | Career Development and Guidance Centre, PEC Chandigarh's own placement cell |
| COEP | College of Engineering, Pune, now formally COEP Technological University, one of the plan's four locked picks |
| CPC | Cost per click, a paid-media pricing unit used in the channel benchmarks (Lane F) |
| CPL | Cost per lead, the paid-media pricing unit the model actually uses for Meta and LinkedIn channel assumptions (A11, A12) |
| CPM | Cost per thousand impressions, a paid-media pricing unit referenced but not pinned to a single figure in the model (ruling F-C1) |
| CTC | Cost to company, the standard Indian term for a recruiter's total annual compensation offer |
| DAA | Dean of Academic Affairs, the PEC Chandigarh office whose letters set the institute's internship-registration calendar |
| DCG | DTU Consulting Group, co-organiser and co-judge of this case competition, and BeyondTech's current co-branded case-league partner |

| ENE | Environmental Engineering, one of DTU's named non-circuital branch groups |
| ERRC | Eliminate, Reduce, Raise, Create, the Blue Ocean Strategy framework used for the competitive strategy canvas (section 7) |
| Fit score | This plan's own composite college-ranking score (0.25 C1 + 0.15 C2 + 0.15 C3 + 0.15 C4 + 0.10 C5 + 0.10 C6 + 0.10 C7, maximum 5.00), never a published external rating |
| GDGoC | Google Developer Groups on Campus, the 2024 rename of Google Developer Student Clubs (GDSC) |
| GDSC | Google Developer Student Clubs, the pre-2024 name for what is now GDG on Campus |
| IIIT | Indian Institute of Information Technology, as in IIIT Delhi, IIIT Allahabad and IIIT Hyderabad, all three screened out on batch size (F1) |
| IIM | Indian Institute of Management, referenced for its consulting-club casebooks and case-competition precedents (Lane D, Lane G) |
| IIT | Indian Institute of Technology, the four contrast-set institutes (Delhi, Roorkee, BHU Varanasi, Guwahati) shown in the scorecard but excluded from the final four |
| ISP | Internshala Student Partner, Internshala's own campus-ambassador programme, a named precedent in Lane G |
| J&K | Jammu and Kashmir, named specifically in this plan's India-map accuracy requirement (section 14.5) |

| NIT | National Institute of Technology, as in NIT Tiruchirappalli, NIT Warangal, NIT Surathkal, NIT Rourkela, NIT Kurukshetra, NIT Jalandhar and NIT Calicut, all on the plan's longlist |
