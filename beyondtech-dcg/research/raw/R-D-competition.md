Cannot confirm whether the seed price is current, retired, or now personalized per-lead. Marked CORRECTED in Facts (RD02), not NOT FOUND, because the page was actually fetched and affirmatively shows no price, which is itself informative.
2. **CrackNonTech's social media presence and ambassador/channel structure.** Queries: (a) "CrackNonTech Jitendra Kumar Instagram LinkedIn YouTube followers", (b) "CrackNonTech ambassador campus program ads channels", (c) the general non-tech-placement query (below) also surfaced nothing on CrackNonTech's own channels. NOT FOUND for the exact figure; closest proxy is the aggregator-reported general NextLeap price of about Rs 27,499 (S153), which is not EMI and not PM-Fellowship-specific. See Conflicts.
