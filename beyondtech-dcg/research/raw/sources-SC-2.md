## Notes on method

- Source hierarchy followed per PLAN.md section 6.2: official NIRF filings and institute placement-cell documents were sought first for every college (search pattern "<college> NIRF 2025 engineering data pdf" and "site:<college domain> NIRF"), then placement-cell pages, then aggregators, in that order. Only NIT Tiruchirappalli's and NIT Calicut's official PDFs/notices were directly reachable in this pass; for the other 7 colleges, the official NIRF/CDC filing could not be located as a fetchable document after 3 distinct search attempts each, and the aggregator-reported figure is used instead, labelled "aggregator-reported" in the CSV per the source hierarchy's own instruction.
- NIT Tiruchirappalli's official 2025-26 UG Placement Brochure (S501) was fetched directly (via `curl --insecure`, since WebFetch's certificate check failed on nitt.edu) and its recruiter-wall page was rendered to an image and read visually, since the recruiter names are embedded as logos, not extractable text. This is the only college in this batch where the anchor-recruiter count rests on a direct visual read of an official document rather than an aggregator's own claim.
- No number, recruiter visit, or follower count was invented. Every NOT FOUND cell reflects a minimum of 3 distinct search attempts per PLAN.md section 0.3 DO item 4.
- IIIT Hyderabad is flagged in the CSV as the college most likely to fail the PLAN.md section 7.2 screen (C3 base far under 600; C1 under the 5-anchor F2 threshold on current evidence). VIT Vellore and Manipal Institute of Technology are flagged as having the weakest-sourced C3 (graduating-batch-size) figures in this batch, since both are large private universities whose public reporting emphasises aggregate offer counts over a structured NIRF-style disclosure that this lane could locate.
