## Notes on methodology for this stage
- Every S-ID above is newly fetched/searched at the dossier stage (step B10, SC-3's third pass); facts
  already sourced at the lane or scorecard stage are cited in the four `R-E-*.md` files as "(reused)"
  against their original S600-S649 IDs, per this project's rule against re-researching the same fact
  twice.
- No personal name, phone number or email belonging to a private individual is reproduced in any of
  the four dossier files; where a search surfaced a named person (e.g. a club coordinator, a 180DC
  branch's listed leadership), the dossier cites only the role/title and, where available, an
  institutional mailbox (e.g. placement@goa.bits-pilani.ac.in, support@trystiitd.org).
- Three distinct searches were run per topic area per college before any cell was marked NOT FOUND, per
  the task's instruction; several additional targeted searches were run beyond the minimum three where
  the task brief specifically asked for a fresh angle (IIT Delhi's consulting/finance club question;
  IIT Roorkee's and IIT Delhi's calendar-fit confirmation).
- The single most significant finding of this dossier stage is RE86/RE87 in `R-E-iit-delhi.md`:
  IIT Delhi does have named DMS-level consulting (Consulere) and finance (FINAZARDS) clubs that publish
  case booklets, a materially different finding from the scorecard-stage "0 of 3" null result. This is
  logged as a refinement, not a reversal, because the clubs are confirmed DMS (MBA-equivalent)
  affiliated and the B.Tech-undergraduate reach question remains open, consistent with the identical
  caveat already applied to IIT Roorkee's Samadhan/Vittarth at the scorecard stage.
