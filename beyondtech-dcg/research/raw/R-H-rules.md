# R-H: Rules that constrain the claims and the tactics
Prepared 10 Oct 2026 by V-H. Access date for every URL: 10 Oct 2026.

Note on method: the official CCPA and ASCI PDFs could not be read as text (image-based or
binary-only renders; see sources S304, S307, S311). Every clause below that reads as a quoted rule
is therefore sourced to a law-firm or news secondary summary of the primary text, not to the
primary text itself, and is marked accordingly. No clause, guideline or legal requirement is
invented; where a secondary source paraphrases rather than quotes, that is stated. This lane
describes what sources say; it does not give legal advice or state a compliance conclusion as
certain.
