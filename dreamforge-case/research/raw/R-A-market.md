## Conflicts
- C-A1: India quick commerce market size disagrees sharply across sources: Zerodha's Daily Brief (12 Jun 2026) puts it at about USD 11 billion, while ZiroMarket puts the GMV run rate at about USD 30 billion with Blinkit at about 45% share. The two likely measure different bases (for example, annualised GOV run-rate versus a narrower revenue or category definition), but neither source states its exact methodology clearly enough to reconcile them. Ruling: both kept, Zerodha preferred as the primary deck figure because it is tied to a dated, sourced quarter (Q4 FY26) rather than an undated run-rate claim; logged to raw/conflicts.md as C-A1.
- C-A2: Zepto's FY25 revenue is reported as ₹11,110 crore (+149% year on year) by one IPO tracker and as ₹9,669 crore (+129% year on year) by a second. Neither cites the DRHP page number. Ruling: both kept pending DRHP verification; logged to raw/conflicts.md as C-A2.
- C-A3: Shadowfax's largest-client revenue concentration is given as 48.91% (six months to 30 Sep 2025) by one secondary source and as 48% (FY25) by a second, with the FY23 comparator given as 59.52% by the first source only. These are close enough to probably be the same underlying DRHP figure rounded differently, but the period labels (six months vs full year) do not match exactly. Ruling: both kept as approximately consistent, flagged for verifier reopen against the DRHP; logged to raw/conflicts.md as C-A3.
- C-A4: Blinkit's dark store total in Delhi-NCR-relevant national coverage is given as 1,301 after Q4 FY25 (Mar 2025) by one source, rising to 1,544 by Q1 FY26 and then 2,243 by Q4 FY26 (Mar 2026) in the Zerodha piece.
