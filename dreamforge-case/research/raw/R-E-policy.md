## Conflicts
- C-E1: Whether the 10 August 2026 PM E-DRIVE amendment (S.O. 4424(E)) extends the e-2W, e-rickshaw and L5 e-3W terminal date to 31 March 2028, or leaves it unchanged at 31 March 2026. The primary gazette text opened directly (S324, RE12 to RE14) shows the amended Table-1/Annexure-4 entry for registered e-2 wheelers carrying a "01.04.2025 to 31.03.2028" column header and the scheme's own para 2 amendment stating the scheme "shall be implemented... till 31st March 2028", which supports the extension applying to e-2W. Motor India Online (S351, RE23) states the opposite for e2W, e3W, e-rickshaws and L5, while Autocar India, Autocar Professional, Free Press Journal and CIOL (S320 to S322, S353) all independently report the e-2W extension. Per source precedence (government text over press), this document treats the e-2W extension to 31 March 2028 as the base case and flags Motor India Online's contrary claim for the user.
- C-E2: Free Press Journal (S322) headlines the 10 August 2026 change as a 50% cut to the e-2W subsidy. The primary gazette text amended at RE13 to RE14 only raises the vehicle cap (to 45,79,120) and the fund figure (to ₹2,767 crore); it does not restate or change the per kWh rate or per vehicle cap, which were already set at ₹2,500 per kWh capped at ₹5,000 from FY 2025-26 onward by the original 2024 notification (RE03). The "50% cut" framing appears to describe the FY 2024-25 to FY 2025-26 step-down that happened under the original 2024 notification, not a fresh change made in August 2026. Also appended to raw/conflicts.md.
- C-E3: Delhi minimum wage for unskilled workers is reported as 18,456 rupees a month, effective 1 April 2025 (DCPSOL, S343, with a specific notification number, and ClearTax, S344, though ClearTax mislabels the effective year), against a higher 19,846 rupees a month figure attributed to a 1 October 2025 revision (Zimyo, S345), which a fourth source (wage.is, S346) says was a forged order disavowed by the Delhi Labour Department. This document relies on the 18,456 rupee figure as the better-sourced one. Also appended to raw/conflicts.md.
- C-E4: CAQM Direction No. 94 (S333 to S335, RE51) sets 1 January 2026 as the date from which new petrol two-wheelers cannot be added to covered NCR delivery fleets. The Delhi EV Policy 2026 gazette text (S328, RE37) separately carves out an exception permitting induction of BS-VI two-wheelers into the existing fleet up to 31 December 2026, a full year later than the CAQM direction's headline date. Both are primary or primary-adjacent government texts; the document should read the Delhi EV Policy 2026 clause as the operative, more specific and more recent carve-out for BS-VI two-wheelers in Delhi NCT, while noting the CAQM direction's own January 2026 date applies more broadly across the wider NCR.
