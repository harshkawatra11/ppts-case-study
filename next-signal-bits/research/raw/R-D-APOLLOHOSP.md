# R-D-APOLLOHOSP: Apollo Hospitals Enterprise Limited
Accessed: 11 Oct 2026 to 13 Oct 2026. Researcher: lane agent R-D-APOLLOHOSP.

Note on company type: Apollo Hospitals Enterprise Limited (AHEL) is a hospital-chain and omnichannel
pharmacy and digital-health group, not a pharma manufacturer. The metrics that matter here are beds,
occupancy, ARPOB (average revenue per occupied bed) and segment PAT across three reported segments:
Healthcare Services (hospitals), Apollo HealthCo (pharmacy distribution plus Apollo 24/7 digital health),
and Apollo Health and Lifestyle Limited (AHLL, clinics, diagnostics, Cradle maternity, fertility). This
file follows the symmetric company-dossier template at PLAN.md paragraph 4.2 ("R-D Company dossiers"),
adapted for a hospital-playbook company as the lane brief instructs.
