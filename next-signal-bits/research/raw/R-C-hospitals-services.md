## What this section establishes, and what it does not

This lane establishes directional and company-level evidence that Indian hospital economics (ARPOB, occupancy, ALOS, EBITDA per bed) are improving gradually across most listed chains through FY25 and FY26 even as occupancy often dips at newly commissioned capacity (KIMS, Rainbow, Jupiter all show this pattern explicitly), that announced bed-expansion targets are revised upward over time company by company (Apollo's bed-addition plan became 5,800 beds; Max's targets range from 3,500 to 10,000 beds across different company statements within the same year), and that the regulatory environment (CGHS October 2025 revision, PMJAY package-rate ceilings materially below private charges, an unconfirmed-as-current NPPA device price cap) continues to pressure government-scheme economics relative to cash and private-insurance payors, which several chains are visibly shifting toward. The re-verification pass materially strengthened the evidentiary basis for most of these claims (54 of 94 rows are now VERIFIED or CORRECTED, versus 9 before this pass) and, in a handful of cases, caught and corrected figures that could not survive a second, independent read of the cited source (Apollo's bed-plan numbers, Fortis's ARPOB growth rate, Medanta's Noida loss figure, MedPlus's EBITDA-margin label, the CGHS OPD fee baseline, Metropolis's RPP/RPT period) — exactly the kind of correction this pass was designed to surface. It does not establish a clean, source-verified payor-mix table for any single company across any full year, a confirmed current NPPA price-cap status for stents or knee implants (and the specific stent rupee figures in this file should now be treated with active suspicion pending further check, not merely as an open gap), a reliable, disaggregated brownfield-versus-greenfield capex-per-bed benchmark, or (despite extensive effort) an 85%-plus VERIFIED/CORRECTED share across the full row set, because a meaningful fraction of this lane's sourcing depends on broker-research and rating-agency PDFs that no fetch attempt in either pass could get past binary/compressed-stream extraction; all of this is stated explicitly above rather than filled with an estimate presented as a fact.
