Ruling: favour the completion-stage figures (58.28%, Rs 297.97 crore) as the economically final, more precisely stated, and independently corroborated numbers; the signing-stage figures likely reflect preliminary deal terms that were adjusted before closing, consistent with ordinary M&A practice, not a factual error.

- C-RDM05: Jaypee Healthcare acquisition consideration. Wikipedia (directly fetched) states the 64% stake was acquired at "an enterprise value of Rs 1,660 crore."
