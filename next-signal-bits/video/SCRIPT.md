# Narration: Next Signal product demo

Voice: Harsh Kawatra. Calm, confident, analytical; a strategist walking a judge through one live run. Natural
pace (about 164 words a minute, same convention as the Eurogrip recording). Let numbers land; never rush the
discovery sentence or the strength label. Total 236 words, about 86 seconds of speech; the finished video must
stay under 2 minutes, with dwell time left on the longer live beats for the viewer to actually read the product.

Say the numbers and names like this: "NIFTY fifty" as two words, not an initialism; "Cipla" as "SIP-la"; the six
stage names said as plain English words, each with a brief pause before the next, since beat 3's visual pops one
card per word.

## S01 (cover and thesis, 32 words, about 12s)

I'm Harsh Kawatra, with Gursimran Kaur, Team IdeaForge. Most finance tools summarise a filing. Next Signal
computes first, checks outside the company, and spends most of its effort trying to prove itself wrong.

## S02 (live: home and entry, 13 words, about 5s)

Give it any NIFTY fifty pharma or healthcare company. We give it Cipla.

## S03 (cutaway: six stages, 23 words, about 8s)

Six stages, always in the same order: Discover, Connect, Calculate, Reason, Challenge, Prove. The arithmetic
runs before any language model sees the numbers.

## S04 (live: run filling in, 41 words, about 15s)

Discover reads the filings and the regulator record. Connect pulls every quarter of results straight from the
exchange. Calculate runs the numbers against five years of history and against Cipla's own peers, before anyone
forms an opinion about what they mean.

## S05 (live: report, discovery, strength, 40 words, about 15s)

Reason proposes what changed. Challenge spends more time trying to kill that idea than defending it, against
nine separate tests. What survives gets a strength score, not a guess: strong, moderate or weak, and the
reasoning is shown, not hidden.

## S06 (live: evidence drawer, challenge tab, 32 words, about 12s)

Click any number and it opens the exact filing and the exact quote behind it. Every rival explanation sits in
the same table, scored the same way, so nothing is quietly favoured.

## S07 (live: export research paper, 31 words, about 11s)

The same run exports as a dashboard, a research paper, an Excel workbook, or a CSV bundle, because a discovery
that cannot be checked by someone else is not a discovery.

## S08 (cutaway: closing, 20 words, about 7s)

Public data only. Nothing here is investment advice. The research paper and the model workbook are linked
below. Thank you.
