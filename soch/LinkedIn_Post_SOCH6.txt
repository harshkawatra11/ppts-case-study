3rd place at SOCH 6.0, Enactus DTU's sports innovation ideathon.

40 teams, 7 surprise cases revealed on the day of the competition. We picked ours by chit, not choice, and that was the whole point. No prep, no case in hand beforehand, just a problem statement none of us had seen until 9 am and a hard stop at 1 pm to have a business built and a 5 slide deck ready to submit.

Our case was solar powered sports infrastructure for tier 2 and tier 3 India. Public grounds across the country already exist, thousands of them, but they go dark the moment the sun sets, and the people who most need an evening slot to play, students, working professionals, women, are exactly the ones locked out by that gap.

Four hours in, the model we landed on was simple to say and harder to defend. Don't sell the electricity. Use it to buy back the hour. A unit of solar power sold to the grid is worth very little. The same unit, routed into a floodlight, turns a dead evening into a bookable slot, a coaching session, a local league. That single reframe shaped the pricing, the partnerships with panchayats and schools, and how the whole thing pays for itself.

Out of 40 teams, 9 got shortlisted off the strength of that submission, and we were one of them. Then came an 8 minute presentation and a 2 minute Q&A in front of Enactus DTU's heads and the case mentors, who had clearly read every slide and were not interested in letting a weak assumption slide.

Landing on the podium after building the entire thing in one sitting, under a clock, on a case we saw for the first time that morning, feels like the kind of result you earn in the room and nowhere else.

Grateful to Enactus DTU for running a competition with this much rigor, and to the mentors who pushed us hard in both rounds.

Team: Harsh Kawatra, Anuj Gambhir, Gursimran Kaur, Dayita Arora.
