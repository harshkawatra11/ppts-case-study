## Gaps

- No chilled (short-shelf-life, refrigerated) Indian ready-to-heat meal brand at national retail scale was found to directly benchmark Proposal C's category against (RD41). ITC's ready-to-eat lines are ambient, not chilled; this is a genuine gap in the Indian evidence base, not an inference.
- No exact listing-fee amount, slotting-fee amount or credit-day term (in rupees or days) for Indian modern trade was found in any source searched (RD43, RD47). Figures found are margin percentages only, and mostly dated (2011).
- No current (2024 to 2026) figure for the overall share of private label in Indian modern trade was found; the only Nielsen figure located (4.5%, RD48) is from 2013 reporting on 2012 to 2014 data and is now over a decade old.
- No record of what happened to iChef or Cookfresh after their respective launches was found (RD12, RD14); neither a shutdown date nor a continuation could be confirmed.
- No stated reason for Burgundy Box's current status (active, dormant or closed) was found (RD10).

## Conflicts

- Licious FY24 net loss: one Inc42 headline states ₹294 crore while the same article's body and detailed breakdown states ₹298.6 crore (RD20). The ₹298.6 crore figure is the one carried into the detailed financial table in the article and is the one recorded as the primary figure.
- Licious FY25 revenue: Entrackr's dedicated article states ₹795 crore (RD21, published 18 Oct 2025); a later Inc42 article on the same fiscal year states ₹797.2 crore (RD22). Both are kept on record; the figures differ by about ₹2.2 crore, within normal rounding or reporting-date variance.
- FreshToHome FY24/FY25 revenue direction: Entrackr's dedicated article (RD23) states FY25 revenue of ₹421 crore grew from FY24's ₹369.5 crore. A secondary, derivative summary (RD24) states the reverse, that revenue fell from ₹421.33 crore in FY24 to ₹369.55 crore in FY25. The years appear to be transposed in the secondary source. RD23, from the primary financial-reporting outlet, is treated as the more reliable figure.
- Country Delight valuation: one data aggregator states $731.1 million as of 27 March 2025; another states $758 million tied to its Series E round (RD26). Both figures are kept on record; neither could be traced to a company-issued statement in this search.
- Chef's Basket's 2015 Series A investor is named as SAIF Partners in the Inc42 funding story (RD02) and as Elevation Capital in the CBInsights comparison page (RD03). Elevation Capital is SAIF Partners India's later rebranded name (the rebrand took effect in 2019, after the 2015 round), so this is likely the same entity referred to by its current and former names rather than a true factual conflict, but both source wordings are kept on record since neither source states the rebrand explicitly.
