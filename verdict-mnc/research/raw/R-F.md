## Gaps

- F2 is the thinnest lane. No primary, quantified, meal-kit-specific study of brand dilution from selling a premium D2C food brand through grocery was found. The three rows recorded (RF17 to RF19) are either a stated absence of evidence or general, non-food, non-India case-study material from secondary compilation sites, and should be used only as illustrative analogy, never as an evidenced HarvestBox-specific risk figure.
- F5 is similarly thin for India specifically. No Nielsen, Kantar or NielsenIQ India chilled-category velocity benchmark (units or rupees per store per week) was located. The one velocity figure found (RF32, GBP 500 to 600 a week) is UK-only and from a search summary, not a direct document read.
- Several Albertsons/Plated rows (RF08 to RF12, RF29 to RF31) could not be independently confirmed by direct fetch in this session: boisedev.com, progressivegrocer.com, storebrands.com, andnowuknow.com and the SEC EDGAR filing all returned HTTP 403 Forbidden to the fetch tool used. These facts are carried from search-engine result summaries only and are marked UNVERIFIABLE pending a direct read, though several corroborate each other (RF10 and RF11 on the November 2019 timing; RF08 and RF09 on the September 2017 acquisition).
- No stated commercial reason (underperformance, cost, specific loss figure) for Albertsons winding down Plated's home-delivery business in November 2019 was found in any source opened or summarised in this session. Per the plan's instruction for Lane D's equivalent gap, this should be written into the research document as "no stated reason found," not inferred.
- No single current (2024 or 2025), India-specific percentage figure for private-label share of modern trade overall was found; RF20 and RF21 are dated to a 2012/2013 reference period, while RF22 to RF27 (2025 and FY24) are company- and consumer-survey-level, not an aggregate modern-trade percentage. A writer using these together must state both time periods and not imply a single current aggregate share.
- RF15 and RF16 (Sainsbury's-Gousto and Waitrose-Mindful Chef partnerships) carry no confirmed date; both were accessed only through search-engine summaries of paywalled or unopened articles.
- DMart's private-label revenue share was searched for specifically but no figure was returned in any search result in this session; only BigBasket and Zepto figures were found.

## Conflicts

- **Why Plated's in-store trial failed versus why its home-delivery business was wound down.** RF12 (headline only, not opened) suggests Albertsons pulled Plated kits from some store shelves as early as March 2019, which would predate and be distinct from the November 2019 announcement (RF10) that phased out the home-delivery subscription business specifically while expanding the in-store private-label range. These are two different events (an in-store pullback versus a delivery-business wind-down) that secondary coverage sometimes conflates. Both are recorded separately here; a writer should not treat them as the same event without reading the full BoiseDev coverage directly.
- **India private-label share: historical (4.5% overall, RF20) versus current, category-specific signals (RF22 to RF27).** The only aggregate modern-trade percentage found is over a decade old. The 2025 EY survey (RF22) measures consumer switching behaviour and attitudes, not a market-share percentage, and BigBasket's ~40% (RF25) is one e-commerce grocer's own private-label share of its own sales, not a modern-trade-wide figure. These three data points describe different things (a 2012 market-share estimate, a 2025 attitudinal survey, and one retailer's internal mix) and should not be combined into a single "current Indian private-label share" statistic in the research document.
